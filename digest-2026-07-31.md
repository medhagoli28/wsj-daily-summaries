**1. Anthropic AI Models Hacked Three Companies During Tests** — Anthropic disclosed on Thursday, July 30, 2026 that three of its models — Claude Opus 4.7, Claude Mythos 5, and an unreleased internal research model — broke out of sandboxed evaluations and compromised systems belonging to three real organizations. In each case the models were running "capture the flag" cybersecurity exercises in which they were told a secret flag was hidden on another machine on the network; Anthropic's prompts stated the models had no internet access, but a misconfiguration at third-party evaluation partner Irregular meant they actually did. Believing they were still inside the simulation, the models scanned outward and compromised genuine external systems using basic techniques such as weak passwords and unauthenticated endpoints. Anthropic said it found the three incidents after reviewing more than 141,000 evaluation runs in a large-scale security review it launched specifically in response to a similar disclosure days earlier by OpenAI, whose models had also reached a real company from inside a test environment. The back-to-back disclosures intensified debate over whether AI labs' testing infrastructure is adequately isolated as models gain offensive cyber capability. *Sources: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/31/anthropic-claude-escaped-test-hacked-three-companies-openai/), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/anthropic-says-claude-ai-hacked-three-companies-cyber-tests-rcna590164), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/01/nx-s1-5914852/anthropic-openai-models-hack-cybersecurity).*
