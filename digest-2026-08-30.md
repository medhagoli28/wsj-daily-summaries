## Tech

- **Three Takeaways From Bill Gates’s 5,784-Word Warning on AI: ‘There Is No Plan’** — Bill Gates published a 5,784-word essay on his personal website on August 26, 2026, warning that society has "no plan" for the disruption AI will cause, calling the coming transition "one of the most turbulent times in human history." Gates, once known as an AI optimist, wrote that both white- and blue-collar jobs will vanish within a decade without historical precedent, hitting entry-level positions hardest, and he proposed a "Human Reserved" concept in which governments set aside certain jobs for people only and tax automation to fund retraining and a stronger safety net. He also raised concerns about AI's effects on child development and crime, and urged governments to build new institutions before unemployment rises sharply and public trust erodes. The essay was covered by multiple outlets, including Fortune and Yahoo Finance, as a notable shift from Gates's earlier, more optimistic stance on the technology. _Sources: <https://fortune.com/2026/08/26/bill-gates-ai-warning-risks-benefit-world-leaders/>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/3-key-takeaways-bill-gates-090540503.html>, <https://www.newser.com/story/395317/bill-gates-issues-a-6k-word-ai-warning.html>_

## Markets & Finance

- **Major U.S. Stock Indexes Slip Amid Mideast Flare-Up** — U.S. stock indexes fell on Monday, August 31, 2026, after renewed fighting between the U.S. and Iran rattled markets, with the S&P 500 slipping about 0.43-0.45%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropping roughly 0.56-0.60%, and the Nasdaq Composite losing around 0.34-0.38%. The selloff followed U.S. strikes on Iranian rocket launchers near the Strait of Hormuz that were allegedly preparing to lay mines, with Iran and its allies responding with counterstrikes. Oil prices jumped on the escalation, with Brent crude climbing back above $90 a barrel amid fears the conflict could disrupt shipments through the critical Hormuz shipping route. Despite the Monday pullback, Wall Street was on track to close out August with monthly gains, led by strong tech earnings, even as Middle East tensions produced choppy trading throughout the month. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html>, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/s-p-500-falls-amid-oil-spike-as-middle-east-conflict-intensifies>, <https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-31-2026>_
- **Corporate America’s Profits Are Booming—and Signal More Good Times Ahead** — S&P 500 companies posted second-quarter 2026 earnings up roughly 31% year over year, according to LSEG data compiled by Bloomberg Intelligence and others—well above the ~23% growth analysts had forecast and marking the seventh straight quarter of double-digit profit gains, per Benzinga's coverage of FactSet data. About 88% of reporting companies beat EPS estimates, versus a five-year average of 78%, with results coming in 16.4% above estimates on average. Commerce Department data cited by Bloomberg showed after-tax corporate profits as a share of gross value added hit 19.4% in Q2, the highest level in records dating to the 1940s, while FactSet separately reported the S&P 500's blended net profit margin reached 16.9%, its highest since FactSet began tracking the metric in 2009. Analysts now project full-year 2026 S&P 500 earnings growth of about 30%, with double-digit gains expected to continue into Q3 and Q4. _Sources: <https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/08/61075408/sp-500-earnings-growth-q2-2026-factset>, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/us-corporate-profit-margins-widen-to-highest-on-record>, <https://insight.factset.com/sp-500-reporting-highest-net-profit-margin-in-more-than-15-years-1>_

## Personal Finance

- **Exclusive | Andreessen Horowitz Launches ‘Machine Age’ Fund to Tackle AI Supply Bottlenecks** — Andreessen Horowitz raised $1.1 billion for a new "Machine Age" fund, announced August 28, 2026, marking the venture firm's first vehicle dedicated specifically to AI's physical infrastructure layer rather than software. The fund will back semiconductors, memory chips, networking gear, data storage, data centers, robotics, and other hardware needed to run AI systems, aiming to "accelerate the physical buildout of AI." Five a16z partners are leading the effort — Ben Horowitz, Martin Casado, Raghu Raghuram, David Ulevitch, and David George — who argue that hardware supply, historically growing 20-30% a year, can't keep pace with triple-digit growth in AI compute demand. The move represents a strategic shift for a16z, long known for software bets like backing SpaceX and Anduril, into direct investment in the chips, power, and physical systems underpinning the AI boom. _Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/>, <https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/andreessen-horowitz-targets-ai-supply-crunch-with-new-1-1-billion-hardware-fund>_
- **Japan Spent Record $98.7 Billion to Prop Up Yen in Joint Move With U.S.** — Japan's Ministry of Finance confirmed it spent a record ¥15.39 trillion (roughly $96-98 billion, depending on the exchange rate used for conversion) on foreign-exchange intervention between July 30 and August 26, 2026, the largest monthly amount ever spent buying yen and selling dollars, according to the Japan Times and France24. The spree included a rare, coordinated intervention with the U.S. Treasury on July 30-31 — the first joint Japan-U.S. yen-buying operation since 1998 — undertaken after the yen slid to roughly 40-year lows against the dollar. Tokyo's finance ministry said it "will not hesitate to conduct further coordinated interventions in the future" if volatility persists. The July-August total pushed Japan's cumulative intervention spending for 2026 above ¥27 trillion, already surpassing the prior full-year record of about ¥15 trillion set in 2024. _Sources: <https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/08/29/markets/japan-record-forex-interventions/>, <https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260828-japan-spent-record-96-bn-in-yen-interventions-ministry>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/yen-intervention-us-japan-trump-bessent-katayama.html>_
- **Exclusive | U.S. Servicemember, KPMG Employee Are Targets of Prediction Markets Crackdown** — Federal prosecutors in Manhattan and Washington, working with the CFTC, are preparing insider-trading charges against a U.S. servicemember accused of earning more than $1 million on Polymarket by wagering on U.S. military operations in Iran and Venezuela, with charges expected as soon as this fall. A separate case targets a KPMG employee suspected of using material nonpublic information from their audit work to bet on Polymarket over whether a specific public company would beat quarterly earnings estimates. These would be the latest in a broadening federal crackdown that, as of late August 2026, had produced only two criminal Polymarket prosecutions, including a Fort Bragg soldier, Gannon Ken Van Dyke, charged in April 2026 with netting over $400,000 by using classified intelligence to bet on the capture of Venezuelan president Nicolás Maduro. _Sources: <https://casinobeats.com/2026/08/28/us-serviceman-kpmg-employee-face-insider-trading-allegations/>, <https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/193632/prediction-market-insider-trading-new-federal-cases>_
