- **FTC Sues Amazon, Alleging It Deceived Advertisers and Drove Up Prices** — The FTC and 22 U.S. states sued Amazon on August 31, 2026, alleging the company secretly manipulated its search-advertising auctions starting around 2018-2019, quietly raising the minimum price advertisers had to pay and, in some cases, entering its own bids to inflate prices while concealing this from advertisers. The lawsuit claims Amazon extracted more than $20 billion from roughly 1.2 million advertisers, nearly half of them small or medium-sized businesses, and that these inflated advertising costs were ultimately passed on to shoppers through higher product prices.
