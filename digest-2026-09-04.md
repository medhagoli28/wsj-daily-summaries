- **AI Spending Is Up. So Is the Guesswork.** — I have enough concrete, non-WSJ-sourced facts to write the brief now. A Teneo survey of more than 350 CEOs at public companies with at least $1 billion in annual revenue, conducted in October-November 2025, found that 68% plan to increase AI spending in 2026, up only two percentage points from a year earlier, even though fewer than half of their current AI projects have generated returns exceeding their initial costs.
