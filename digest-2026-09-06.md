Leveraged and single-stock ETFs, which promise two or three times the daily return of an index or stock, have proven far tougher to hold than to buy: 63 leveraged single-stock ETFs closed in the U.S. through mid-2026, versus just 3 closures in all of 2025, even as a record 244 leveraged ETFs launched by mid-August 2026 (compared with 229 for all of 2025).
