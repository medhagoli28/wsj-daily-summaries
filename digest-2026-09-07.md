## Personal Finance

- **Young People Are Obsessed With This Simple Retirement-Savings Formula** — I have enough concrete detail now to write the brief. Coast FIRE — a formula that calculates how much money someone needs invested at a given age so compound growth alone, with no further contributions, carries them to a full retirement nest egg by around age 65 — has become a viral touchstone among young savers on platforms like TikTok, popularized by creators such as Humphrey Yang, who cite benchmarks like roughly $100,000 saved by age 25 leading to over $1 million by retirement. NerdWallet Wealth Partners CEO Ryan Sterling notes the appeal is that reaching one's "Coast number" frees up income for spending today, such as travel or a home purchase, rather than continuing to save aggressively. Financial planners caution the strategy carries real risks: it depends heavily on assumed market returns, poor performance early in the "coasting" years can derail the projection, and the number is often based on rough spending estimates rather than a person's actual future needs, leaving a gap if circumstances change or someone is laid off. The approach positions itself as a less extreme alternative to traditional FIRE (Financial Independence, Retire Early), letting people ease off retirement savings mid-career while still working. _Sources: <https://www.scrippsnews.com/politics/economy/what-is-coast-fire-for-retirement-and-can-it-work-for-you>, <https://ground.news/article/fire-what-mistakes-make-the-dream-of-early-retirement-burst>_
