- **Is China Stealing American AI? Why ‘Distillation’ Has Washington Up in Arms** — U.S. federal agencies — the NSA, CISA, and FBI — issued a joint advisory on September 8-9, 2026 accusing six Chinese AI firms (DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI) of "industrial-scale" theft of American AI models through a technique called distillation, in which a smaller model is trained to mimic outputs from a larger, more capable one. Anthropic said the Chinese labs generated over 16 million exchanges with its Claude models through roughly 24,000 fraudulent accounts, violating its terms of service and regional access restrictions; officials say the effort dates back to at least late 2024 and was likely carried out with Chinese government awareness. OpenAI and Microsoft separately told the U.S. government in early 2025 that they believed DeepSeek used outputs from GPT models to train its low-cost DeepSeek-R1 model. In response, the White House said it would share threat intelligence with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google to help counter the practice. _Sources: <https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/08/politics/us-accuses-china-of-stealing-ai-technology>, <https://www.govinfosecurity.com/us-warns-chinese-ai-firms-are-illicitly-distilling-models-a-32773>, <https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/us-accuses-china-ai-developers-deepseek-alibaba-copying-american-ai-rcna596696>_

- **Think Your Anonymous Account Is Safe? The entire experiment cost under $2,000, with each account costing between $1 and $4 to identify. In a separate test, the system matched Reddit users across different film-focused communities using only their movie reviews, and boosting the model's reasoning effort roughly doubled the number of correct matches at the strictest confidence threshold.
