- **AI Is Creating New Trust Problems Between Colleagues** — A controlled Atlassian study of 961 U.S. knowledge workers, conducted March 30–April 7, 2026, found that colleagues rated identical work as significantly worse when told AI had helped produce it, judging the creator as 10 times lazier and 24 percentage points less likely to be recommended for a high-visibility project. A companion 1,006-person pulse survey found 96% of knowledge workers admitted submitting AI-generated work without disclosing it, even though 91% of workers say they expect some disclosure of AI use; separately, 43% of workers said they trust a coworker's output less once they learn AI was involved, versus just 20% who trust it more. Researchers describe this as a "transparency dilemma," where employees are punished for honesty about AI use even as employers and peers say they want it. _Sources: <https://www.atlassian.com/blog/ai-at-work/new-research-shows-honesty-about-ai-use-at-work-is-backfiring>, <https://theconversation.com/being-honest-about-using-ai-at-work-makes-people-trust-you-less-research-finds-253590>_
