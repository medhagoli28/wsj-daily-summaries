## Personal Finance

- **The Simple Request That Could Lower Your Mortgage Rate** — Homeowners and buyers with a locked mortgage rate can ask their lender for a "float-down," a one-time adjustment that lowers a locked rate if market rates fall before closing, typically requiring at least a 0.25 to 0.5 percentage-point drop to qualify and costing roughly 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount as a fee. Separately, borrowers can leverage competing offers as a negotiating tool: presenting a rival lender's lower quote, for example 6.625% versus an initial 6.875%, is a common and often successful way to get a lender to match or beat that rate, since lenders' margins above benchmarks like the 10-year Treasury yield leave room to negotiate. Not all lenders offer float-down options, terms vary by company, and the option can generally be exercised only once during the lock period. _Sources: <https://www.cbsnews.com/news/can-you-negotiate-mortgage-rates-2026/>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/mortgages/article/mortgage-rate-float-down-option-191626269.html>_
