## Tech

- **Dario Says AI Should Slow Down. Jensen Wants to Go Full Steam Ahead.** — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly clashed over AI development speed at Salesforce's Dreamforce conference in San Francisco on September 15, 2026, with Huang telling an audience of roughly 12,000 at Moscone Center that the U.S. should move "as fast as we can" and that existing market forces, not new regulation, are enough to keep AI companies in check. The dispute followed a roughly 4,000-word essay Amodei published on September 12 titled "We Must Pace the Frontier," arguing AI capabilities are advancing faster than anyone's ability to understand or control them and proposing a framework for deliberately slowing capability gains. OpenAI's Sam Altman, SpaceX's Elon Musk, and Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis backed Amodei's call for pacing, while Huang and Meta's Mark Zuckerberg rejected it, framing the split as a broader industry divide between cautious governance and accelerated innovation. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/nvidia-and-anthropic-ceos-diverge-on-ai-safety-at-dreamforce.html>, <https://qz.com/jensen-huang-ai-safety-dreamforce-anthropic-openai-091626>, <https://www.techradar.com/pro/were-going-through-a-new-industrial-revolution-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-and-anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-share-differing-views-on-ai-at-dreamforce-2026>_
- **Howie Buffett Steps In to Preserve What His Father Built** — On September 18, 2026, Warren Buffett, 96, stepped down as chairman of Berkshire Hathaway after leading the company since 1965, becoming chairman emeritus while remaining a board director. His son Howard Buffett, 71, a Berkshire director since 1993, succeeded him as non-executive chairman under the long-standing succession plan, with Greg Abel—who took over as CEO on January 1, 2026—continuing to run the company's operations. Warren Buffett wrote to shareholders that "Greg runs the company; Howard will guard its culture and values – both worth more than anything on our balance sheet," and Howard's brother Peter said he "feels a responsibility to preserve our father's legacy." _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/18/buffett-stepping-down-as-berkshire-chairman.html>, <https://www.axios.com/2026/09/18/warren-buffett-berkshire-hathaway-chairman>_
- **‘Sketchy AF’: What to Know About How OpenAI Staff Discussed Book-Pirating** — Newly unsealed internal Slack messages from the authors' lawsuit against OpenAI (a copyright case now consolidated in Manhattan federal court) show OpenAI employees privately acknowledging in 2019-2021 that using the pirated-book repository LibGen as a training-data source was "sketchy AF," a phrase used by GPT-3 engineers Tom Brown and Ben Mann; Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, then an OpenAI researcher, similarly flagged the practice as "sketchy" and worried about disclosing the datasets' names (Books1, Books2) publicly. The filings also describe "Project Clear" (originally named "excise-libgen" before being renamed by then-General Counsel Jason Kwon), an internal effort in summer 2022 to delete the LibGen files from OpenAI's systems. Plaintiffs' attorneys argue the communications show OpenAI executives knew the piracy was both illegal and likely to harm authors, which could support a finding of willful infringement and statutory damages up to $150,000 per work, versus the standard $750. _Sources: <https://lunch.publishersmarketplace.com/2026/09/openai-admits-pirating-books-from-libgen-was-sketchy-af-kept-a-central-library-just-like-anthropic/>, <https://authorsguild.org/news/ag-v-openai-top-execs-knew-mass-book-piracy-was-illegal/>_

## Markets & Finance

- **‘Iris van Herpen: Sculpting the Senses’ Review: Fashioning a Singular World** — “Iris van Herpen: Sculpting the Senses” is now showing at the Brooklyn Museum in New York from May 16 to December 5, 2026, marking the North American debut of the Dutch designer's retrospective. The show features more than 140 haute couture creations organized into 11 thematic sections, displayed alongside contemporary artworks, scientific specimens, fossils, and sound and video installations. Van Herpen, whose work draws on biomimicry, fractal geometry, and neuroscience and has been worn by Beyoncé, Björk, and Lady Gaga, pioneered 3D-printed couture with her 2010 "Crystallization" collection. The exhibition previously traveled to the Musée des Arts Décoratifs in Paris (2023-24), Queensland Art Gallery in Brisbane, Singapore's ArtScience Museum, and Kunsthal Rotterdam before its Brooklyn opening, which is timed to the museum's May 16, 2026 Brooklyn Artists Ball honoring Van Herpen. _Sources: <https://www.brooklynmuseum.org/exhibitions/iris-van-herpen>, <https://fashionista.com/news/iris-van-herpen-sculpting-the-senses-exhibit-brooklyn-museum-review-interview>, <https://www.timeout.com/newyork/news/brooklyn-museum-is-opening-a-wild-futuristic-fashion-exhibition-next-week-051226>_
- **Burned Out and Unemployed, Young People in China Are Launching AI Startups** — More than seven million new solo companies were registered in China last year, up roughly 42% from 2024, as young people squeezed out of a brutal job market turn to one-person startups powered by AI tools for coding, marketing, and customer service. Rather than raising venture funding and hiring teams, these founders use generative AI to handle tasks a staff would normally cover, letting a single person run an entire company from a laptop. The trend is fueled by both high youth unemployment and burnout from grueling corporate "996" work culture, pushing many to avoid a return to traditional office jobs. However, profitability remains elusive for most: reports indicate more than half of these AI-enabled solo ventures earn under $1,000 a month. _Sources: <https://www.newsdirectory3.com/unemployed-and-burned-out-youth-in-china-launch-ai-startups-amid-job-crisis/>, <https://www.socialnews.xyz/2026/09/22/unemployed-chinese-youth-launching-solo-ai-startups-in-a-brutal-market-report/>_
