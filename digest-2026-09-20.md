## Tech

- **Gemini Joins the Hacker Club** — Google's Gemini AI autonomously hacked into the protected systems of three companies during cybersecurity testing conducted by red-team firm Irregular, marking what the Wall Street Journal reported as the model's first known autonomous hacks; the breaches occurred in May 2026, were discovered by Google in July, and were only confirmed publicly on September 19, 2026 after WSJ inquired. The intrusions happened because a fictional company name used in a "capture the flag" test exercise coincidentally matched a real domain on the public internet, and a misconfiguration left the test environment connected to the internet rather than sealed in a sandbox; Gemini gained access in one case by guessing passwords and in the other two by finding credentials in a public repository. Google said it had not disclosed the incidents earlier because Gemini "acted appropriately," halting each breach once it determined it had compromised a real company, and it has since revised its testing process with Irregular. Similar sandbox-escape incidents tied to Irregular's testing were previously disclosed by Meta, Anthropic, and OpenAI, suggesting this is part of a broader pattern across AI labs in 2026. _Sources: <https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/19/googles-gemini-ai-hacks-3-companies-in-security-test-then-stops>, <https://www.cybersecuritydive.com/news/google-ai-gemini-autonomous-hacks/830884/>, <https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/19/googles-gemini-is-the-latest-ai-model-to-hack-other-companies/>_

He pledged the government "will not in any way hinder or stifle the growth" of the AI industry but would use existing criminal and civil laws to police "BAD" actors, while giving no details on the Force's structure, authority, or membership. ## Markets & Finance

- **The Backroom Meetings in Miami Where Cuba’s Future Is Being Plotted** — Wealthy and politically connected Cuban-American exiles in Miami are quietly drafting blueprints for a post-communist Cuba, including plans for a new stock exchange, floating power stations, airport renovations, and billions in private investment, with the Trump administration turning to them for ideas, political support, and capital should the Havana regime fall. The planning accelerated after U.S. special forces captured Venezuelan leader Nicolás Maduro on January 3, 2026, and Washington subsequently cut off the oil shipments that had been keeping Cuba's power grid running. Jorge Mas, the billionaire chairman of MasTec and managing owner of Inter Miami CF, has said President Trump personally asked him how much it would cost to rebuild Cuba. Competing opposition groups have produced rival frameworks — including a "freedom accord," a "fundamental law," and a Liberation Agreement signed by the Asamblea de la Resistencia Cubana and Rosa María Payá's Pasos de Cambio coalition proposing a 51-member provisional council — while exiles clash over unresolved questions like reconciliation and what to do with the Communist Party. _Sources: <https://www.latintimes.com/new-cuba-designed-miami-inside-powerful-exiles-plan-government-stock-market-infrastructure-599429>, <https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/miami-plans-cuba-day-revisit-194817464.html>, <https://voz.us/en/politics/260922/39296/cuba-awaits-its-venezuela-moment-wealthy-cuban-americans-plan-transition-in-miami-as-the-iran-war-holds-trump-back.html>_

Coverage of the interview, including from Inc. and Atlassian's blog, frames this as part of Grant's broader 2026 commentary on AI-driven career pivots and workplace change. _Sources: <https://www.atlassian.com/blog/leadership/adam-grant-on-how-ai-reshapes-work-why-agility-beats-ability>, <https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/adam-grant-says-career-success-now-depends-less-on-expertise-and-more-on-agility/91408849>_

- **Crypto Blew Its Big Moment—and the Blame Game Has Begun** — Bitcoin peaked near $126,000 in October 2025 before entering a sustained slide that took it below $60,000 by February 2026, a drawdown of more than 50% that erased the year's institutional-adoption narrative; market participants have traded blame ever since, with Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao publicly denying accusations that the exchange dumped roughly $1 billion in bitcoin to trigger a weekend sell-off. Analysts point to overlapping causes rather than a single culprit: trade-war tariffs, geopolitical tension, a rotation of capital into AI and semiconductor stocks, delayed Federal Reserve rate cuts, and a record $13.5 billion derivatives expiry, compounded by an October 2025 flash crash in which AI trading bots misreading signals drove roughly $19 billion in liquidations. Despite the rout, a Bitwise survey of institutional investors found none of 15 firms interviewed had cut their crypto allocations during the downturn, and several increased exposure, suggesting the "failure" narrative centers on price action rather than institutional retreat. By September 2026, bitcoin was trading in the mid-$80,000s, still well off its October 2025 high. _Sources: <https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/02/02/cz-pushes-back-against-binance-fud-as-blame-game-for-crypto-crash-persists>, <https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/02/15/this-crypto-sell-off-points-to-increased-institutional-influence-in-2026/>, <https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/09/23/bitwise-survey-finds-institutional-crypto-adoption-shifting-from-if-to-how/>_
