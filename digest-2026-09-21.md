- **These Tiny Startups Are Getting Even Smaller With Help From AI** — AI-native startups now run about 25% leaner than pre-AI-era peers, employing fewer junior staff and middle managers, according to a joint Harvard Business School and INSEAD study cited in recent coverage of the trend. - **Google Fined $463 Million By Irish Data Watchdog Over Location Data Handling** — Ireland's Data Protection Commission fined Google €403 million (about $463 million) on September 21, 2026, following an inquiry into how the company processes location data across Web & App Activity, Location History, and Location Accuracy settings; the investigation began in early 2020. - **The IRS Is Cracking Down on a Favorite Way the Ultrawealthy Pass On Money** — I have enough concrete detail now to write the brief. The IRS has hit Nashville heiress Trisha Elcan and her husband with a $736 million tax bill over their use of grantor-retained annuity trusts (GRATs), a widely used technique that lets wealthy families pass appreciating assets to heirs largely tax-free; the couple is fighting the assessment in U.S. Tax Court (Elcan v. Commissioner, No. 3405-25). Elcan funded the GRATs with holding-company interests tied to HCA Healthcare stock and later repurchased assets from the trusts using interest-bearing promissory notes, with the trustee then forgiving portions of those notes to cover the annuity payments; the IRS argues this note-forgiveness maneuver was an improper "indirect" step that could make the entire $687.5 million originally transferred into the trusts a taxable gift. Estate lawyers say the case, first reported by the Wall Street Journal on September 21, 2026, doesn't threaten the basic GRAT structure itself but signals the IRS is scrutinizing how aggressively taxpayers can push the technique. The dispute comes as the federal estate, gift and generation-skipping tax exemption rises to $15 million per person ($30 million per married couple) starting January 1, 2026 under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. _Sources: <https://taxprofblog.aals.org/2026/09/26/wsj-the-irs-is-cracking-down-on-a-favorite-way-the-ultrawealthy-pass-on-money/>, <https://www.kiplinger.com/taxes/the-irs-is-taking-a-closer-look-at-trusts>_
