- **In 200-Page Report, Cornell Confronts the Crisis in American Higher Education** — A Cornell faculty committee released a 200-plus-page report on September 22-23, 2026, warning that American research universities face an "unprecedented" set of pressures from unstable federal funding, declining public trust, rising costs, and the rapid rise of artificial intelligence. The 18-member Committee on the Future of the American University spent roughly a year consulting more than 6,000 stakeholders—faculty, staff, students, alumni, and outside advisors—through town halls and webinars before issuing its recommendations. The report urges Cornell to diversify revenue sources, rethink its tuition model, and broaden the range of viewpoints on campus, while explicitly rejecting a shift to three-year degrees or a major pivot toward online learning. It also argues that universities' sprawling mission across teaching, research, and public service has left them unable to clearly articulate their core purpose to a skeptical public. _Sources: <https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2026/09/future-american-university-report-charts-bold-path-forward>, <https://www.chronicle.com/article/what-ails-higher-ed-elite-colleges-say-the-cure-comes-from-within>, <https://www.newser.com/story/396912/cornell-issues-sweeping-plan-to-tackle-crisis-in-higher-education.html>_
