## Personal Finance

- **Exclusive | BlackRock Investment Portfolios Debut on the Blockchain as Digital Tokens** — Ondo Finance launched three tokenized model portfolios built on investment strategies developed by BlackRock — Ondo High Income, Ondo Diversified Growth, and Ondo High Growth — announced around September 24-25, 2026, each holding a blend of tokenized stock, bond, and Bitcoin ETF exposure. Ondo Global Markets issues the portfolio tokens while Ondo Finance handles tokenization and rebalancing, allowing holdings to be verified onchain and traded around the clock; investors buying a token trigger automatic purchases of the underlying ETF shares. Token holders don't get direct shareholder rights in the underlying securities, but the tokens can be transferred, traded 24/7, and used as collateral in lending protocols. The products are launching first for investors outside the United States, marking one of BlackRock's furthest steps yet into putting portfolio strategies directly on a blockchain, building on its earlier BUIDL tokenized fund work with Securitize. _Sources: <https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/ondo-brings-blackrock-portfolio-strategies-172534213.html>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/blackrock-tokenize-three-investment-portfolios-154800419.html>, <https://www.blockhead.co/2026/09/25/ondo-launches-tokenised-model-portfolios-built-on-blackrock-strategies/>_
