Samsung Commits $1.0 Billion to AI Infrastructure Firm Backed by KKR, Nvidia
Samsung Electronics and five affiliates — Samsung C&T, Samsung SDS, Samsung SDI, Samsung Life Insurance, and Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance — agreed to jointly invest $1 billion in Helix Digital Infrastructure, with Samsung Electronics contributing $500 million, the companies said on September 29, 2026. Helix, launched in June 2026 and led by former AWS chief Adam Selipsky alongside KKR's Waldemar Szlezak, builds AI infrastructure spanning data centers, power generation and transmission, and fiber-optic networks; its other founding backers include KKR, Nvidia, the Kuwait Investment Authority, and power company Vistra. The new commitment adds to more than $10 billion already pledged to the venture.

Google Appeals EU Requests on Android AI Access, Search Data Sharing
Google filed appeals with the Luxembourg-based Court of Justice of the European Union on September 29, 2026, challenging two European Commission orders issued in July under the Digital Markets Act. One order requires Google to give rival AI assistants the same access as Gemini to 11 Android phone features, including voice activation and in-app actions, by August 2027; the other requires Google to share search data with competing search engines by January 2027. Google's senior director for competition, Oliver Bethell, said the company would be forced to "share people's private search history without sufficient anonymisation and weaken vital security protections on Android," warning that mandating access to queries on topics like medical worries or personal relationships "would cause irreversible harm to user privacy."

How Long Will the AI Boom Last? Memory Stocks Offer Clues
Memory-chip stocks have become 2026's hottest AI trade, with Sandisk up roughly 558%, Micron more than doubling (up about 137%), and Seagate up around 172% year-to-date as AI data-center demand collides with tight supply. Samsung and SK Hynix have raised server DRAM prices by up to 70% in Q1 2026 alone, building on roughly 50% increases in 2025, while TrendForce measured conventional DRAM contract prices jumping 93-98% quarter-over-quarter in Q1; Micron says its entire 2026 HBM allocation is already sold out under fixed-price contracts. SK Hynix executives have warned the supply crunch could persist past 2030, with 2027 potentially the tightest year in the industry's history, as the three dominant suppliers (Samsung ~38%, SK Hynix ~29%, Micron ~22% of DRAM share) funnel the bulk of new capacity toward high-bandwidth memory for AI rather than conventional chips.

Japan Finance Minister Reaffirms U.S. Coordination on Yen Stability
Japan's Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama held a call with U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent on September 25, 2026, in which both sides reaffirmed that yen undervaluation is a shared concern and agreed to strengthen bilateral cooperation to keep foreign-exchange markets orderly. The reaffirmation follows a period of unusually close U.S.-Japan coordination on the currency: in July 2026 the two countries conducted their first joint yen-buying intervention in 28 years, and Japan intervened on its own several more times over the spring and summer to support the currency. Katayama has repeatedly told reporters that "generally speaking, yen undervaluation is a problem," signaling Tokyo's readiness to keep working with Washington on FX stability.

Etsy Aims to Get More Personal and Boost Business With AI
Etsy CEO Kruti Patel Goyal, who took over in January 2026 after 15 years at the company, is centering the turnaround on a three-pronged AI push: making the marketplace more personal, more discoverable, and building conversational shopping agents for buyers and sellers, including a "Gift Mode" assistant that asks questions to help shoppers find presents. More than three-quarters of Etsy's push notifications and emails are now personalized using on-platform machine learning, up from less than a quarter previously, and in September Etsy became the first live retail partner for ChatGPT's instant-checkout feature, letting users buy Etsy items directly through OpenAI's platform. The strategy appears to be showing early results, with Q1 2026 revenue reaching $631 million (marketplace revenue up over 6% to nearly $433 million) and gross merchandise sales rising more than 5% year-over-year to $2.5 billion, while net income grew by $33 million to $105 million.
