Amex Adds AI, Expense Tracking to Corporate Card to Counter Fintech Rivals
American Express unveiled the next generation of Amex Corporate on September 30, 2026, adding a new Amex Expense mobile app that uses AI to auto-fill expense report fields and cross-check uploaded receipt photos against transaction details, aiming to compete with fintech rivals like Ramp and Brex. The launch also includes new Amex Corporate Cashback Cards and integrated expense-management software bundled directly into the corporate card program. Amex said it plans to roll out additional AI agents later this year, including one that proactively contacts employees for missing expense details and another that lets finance teams query company spending data directly.

Now We’re All Starting to Talk Like AI Chatbots Too
A Florida State University study analyzed 22.1 million words from unscripted spoken content, including science and technology podcasts, and found a measurable rise in AI-associated buzzwords in everyday speech since ChatGPT's late-2022 launch. Words disproportionately favored by large language models—such as "delve," "garner," "underscore," "meticulous," "intricate," and "realm"—have jumped in usage in spontaneous human conversation, while close synonyms like "accentuate" haven't seen the same increase. Researchers say this pattern suggests AI chatbots are influencing spoken vocabulary, not just written text, potentially accelerating linguistic change in a way that likely wouldn't have happened without widespread chatbot use. The findings were first reported in August 2025 and have since been covered by outlets including The Washington Post and Gizmodo.

Oura Postpones IPO Over Market Uncertainty
Oura, the Finnish smart-ring maker, postponed its planned Nasdaq IPO on September 29, 2026, citing broader "uncertainty" in the US market as rising bond yields and interest rates weighed on new listings. The offering had targeted a valuation of up to $2.2 billion and reportedly drew about four times as many orders as available shares, despite the pullback. The company's underlying business remains strong, with revenue expected to grow 90% year-over-year in fiscal 2026 and paid membership reaching 5.7 million following the launch of the Oura Ring 5. Oura joins a growing list of companies delaying US listings in September 2026 amid the shakier market backdrop.

The Gap Between the Rich and the Very, Very Rich Is Getting Wider
The 29 richest people on Earth held a combined $4.1 trillion in 2025, equal to 27% of all billionaire wealth—a concentration that has grown sharply since the 1990s as centi-millionaires and billionaires have seen their fortunes expand far faster than other wealthy households. Billionaire wealth as a whole rose roughly three times faster than its average annual growth rate over the prior five years in 2025, while the U.S. wealthiest 1% now hold about $55 trillion in assets, roughly matching the combined wealth of the bottom 90% of Americans. Separately, the ranks of the "ultra-rich"—those with at least $30 million—grew to about 1% of the world's millionaire population by mid-2025 but already control 32% of the wealth held among all millionaires, underscoring a widening split even within the ranks of the already-wealthy.

Could There Be a Run on the Bond Market?
Hedge funds now hold a record share of the roughly $30 trillion U.S. Treasury market, with their cash Treasury holdings reaching about $2 trillion at the end of 2025—nearly triple the level of five years earlier—as traditional long-term buyers pull back, according to CNBC's reporting on September 30, 2026. Much of that exposure comes via the leveraged cash-futures "basis trade," which the Federal Reserve says has driven the surge in hedge fund Treasury positions, with aggregate basis-trade volumes hitting roughly $830 billion in September 2025, nearly double their 2020 peak. Analysts and regulators, including the IMF's April 2026 Global Financial Stability Report, warn that this reliance on short-term repo funding leaves the market more exposed to a sudden, disorderly deleveraging that could spike Treasury yield volatility; Bloomberg reported on September 22, 2026 that hedge funds have since trimmed basis-trade positions as arbitrage gaps narrowed. Separately, the 10-year Treasury yield rose 45 basis points in September 2026 to its highest level since late 2024, underscoring the market's fragility.

Stock Futures Rise as Oil Prices, Rate Hike Bets Ease
Dow Jones futures rose about 0.39% to roughly 51,890 and S&P 500 futures gained around 0.23% to near 7,750 during European trading on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, as softer Federal Reserve rate-hike expectations lifted sentiment. The CME FedWatch Tool showed the probability of an October rate hike falling sharply to about 45%, down from 70.9% the day before, after New York Fed President John Williams said there was "no need for urgency" on further tightening following the September policy meeting. Oil prices also retreated earlier in the week, easing inflation concerns, amid reports of US-Iran discussions on a phased deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and lift the blockade on Iranian ports. US Treasury yields eased as well after climbing to multi-decade highs in the prior session.

Natural Gas Inches Back Toward $3/mmBtu
Natural gas futures climbed above $3.00 per million British thermal units (MMBtu) on September 23, 2026, marking the highest level since July 8, as late-summer heat lifted power-sector demand and narrowed the storage surplus to roughly 3% above the five-year average for the week ending September 18, down from 3.7% the prior week. Prices touched around $3.11/MMBtu by September 29, supported by strong LNG feedgas demand and a pipeline outage that tightened regional supply. Lighter production also contributed, with a seasonally lean weekly storage build from the EIA helping propel futures to their highest levels in nearly two months after prices had languished below $3 for much of the summer.

HANetf Launches First Euro-Hedged Bitcoin Fund
HANetf, the $9.2 billion European ETF provider, launched the Arrow Bitcoin EUR Hedged ETC (ticker: EBTC) on September 29, 2026, listing on Euronext Paris that day and on Deutsche Börse's Xetra from September 30, marking the world's first euro-hedged bitcoin exchange-traded commodity. The product carries a 0.49% expense ratio and uses currency hedging supplied by HSBC to reduce EUR/USD exchange-rate risk for European investors, since bitcoin is typically priced in dollars. It gives euro-denominated investors bitcoin price exposure while dampening the impact of currency swings on returns.
