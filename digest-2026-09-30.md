- **Now We’re All Starting to Talk Like AI Chatbots Too** — A Florida State University study analyzed 22.1 million words from unscripted spoken content, including science and technology podcasts, and found a measurable rise in AI-associated buzzwords in everyday speech since ChatGPT's late-2022 launch. Words disproportionately favored by large language models—such as "delve," "garner," "underscore," "meticulous," "intricate," and "realm"—have jumped in usage in spontaneous human conversation, while close synonyms like "accentuate" haven't seen the same increase. Researchers say this pattern suggests AI chatbots are influencing spoken vocabulary, not just written text, potentially accelerating linguistic change in a way that likely wouldn't have happened without widespread chatbot use. The findings were first reported in August 2025 and have since been covered by outlets including The Washington Post and Gizmodo. _Sources: <https://news.fsu.edu/news/education-society/2025/08/26/on-screen-and-now-irl-fsu-researchers-find-evidence-suggesting-chatgpt-influences-how-we-speak/>, <https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2025/08/20/chatgpt-claude-chatbots-language/>, <https://gizmodo.com/chatbot-dialect-2000696509>_
- **Oura Postpones IPO Over Market Uncertainty** — Oura, the Finnish smart-ring maker, postponed its planned Nasdaq IPO on September 29, 2026, citing broader "uncertainty" in the US market as rising bond yields and interest rates weighed on new listings. ## Markets & Finance

- **The Gap Between the Rich and the Very, Very Rich Is Getting Wider** — The 29 richest people on Earth held a combined $4.1 trillion in 2025, equal to 27% of all billionaire wealth—a concentration that has grown sharply since the 1990s as centi-millionaires and billionaires have seen their fortunes expand far faster than other wealthy households. Billionaire wealth as a whole rose roughly three times faster than its average annual growth rate over the prior five years in 2025, while the U.S. wealthiest 1% now hold about $55 trillion in assets, roughly matching the combined wealth of the bottom 90% of Americans. Separately, the ranks of the "ultra-rich"—those with at least $30 million—grew to about 1% of the world's millionaire population by mid-2025 but already control 32% of the wealth held among all millionaires, underscoring a widening split even within the ranks of the already-wealthy. _Sources: <https://247wallst.com/personal-finance/2026/09/05/29-people-now-control-27-of-all-billionaire-wealth-and-the-gap-is-widening-fast/>, <https://inequality.org/facts/wealth-inequality/>_
- **Could There Be a Run on the Bond Market?** — Hedge funds now hold a record share of the roughly $30 trillion U.S. Treasury market, with their cash Treasury holdings reaching about $2 trillion at the end of 2025—nearly triple the level of five years earlier—as traditional long-term buyers pull back, according to CNBC's reporting on September 30, 2026. Much of that exposure comes via the leveraged cash-futures "basis trade," which the Federal Reserve says has driven the surge in hedge fund Treasury positions, with aggregate basis-trade volumes hitting roughly $830 billion in September 2025, nearly double their 2020 peak. Analysts and regulators, including the IMF's April 2026 Global Financial Stability Report, warn that this reliance on short-term repo funding leaves the market more exposed to a sudden, disorderly deleveraging that could spike Treasury yield volatility; Bloomberg reported on September 22, 2026 that hedge funds have since trimmed basis-trade positions as arbitrage gaps narrowed. Separately, the 10-year Treasury yield rose 45 basis points in September 2026 to its highest level since late 2024, underscoring the market's fragility. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/us-treasury-market-is-relying-more-on-hedge-funds.html>, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/hedge-funds-pull-back-from-favorite-trade-as-bond-gaps-vanish>, <https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/decomposing-hedge-funds-u-s-treasury-exposures-20260622.html>_

- **HANetf Launches First Euro-Hedged Bitcoin Fund** — HANetf, the $9.2 billion European ETF provider, launched the Arrow Bitcoin EUR Hedged ETC (ticker: EBTC) on September 29, 2026, listing on Euronext Paris that day and on Deutsche Börse's Xetra from September 30, marking the world's first euro-hedged bitcoin exchange-traded commodity.
