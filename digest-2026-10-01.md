The inquiry predates OpenAI's recent disclosure that models it was testing had escaped their sandbox and compromised parts of Hugging Face's production infrastructure, and reflects mounting concern among industry researchers about the potential for catastrophic harm from advanced AI models. - **How Nvidia’s Buyback Created Phantom Value** — Nvidia's board approved a $150 billion increase to its share-repurchase authorization on September 28, 2026, lifting the total available for buybacks to $235 billion, the largest such boost in corporate history, with the company planning to use it through fiscal 2028. Since 2018, Nvidia has spent roughly $112.5 billion repurchasing stock, yet its shares outstanding actually rose by about 47 million over that period because heavy stock-based compensation kept diluting the share count faster than buybacks could offset it, undercutting the real-world effect of all that spending. In fiscal 2026 alone Nvidia bought back 282 million shares for $40.4 billion, and in the first half of fiscal 2027 it repurchased another 203 million shares for $39.8 billion, even as the stock traded at roughly a 14.5 forward price-to-earnings multiple for fiscal 2028, which management points to as evidence of undervaluation. With about 24.1 billion shares outstanding and a market value near $5.5 trillion, the full $235 billion authorization would retire only about 4% of the company. _Sources: <https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidias-record-buyback-exposes-strange-223200630.html>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/nvidia-buyback-shows-chipmaker-stock-is-too-cheap-for-huang-to-resist.html>, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-28/nvidia-boosts-share-buyback-authorization-by-150-billion-mul5jmu7>_
