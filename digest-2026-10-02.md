## Personal Finance

- **Four Big IRA Mistakes That You Can’t Undo** — Several IRA mistakes are irreversible once made: non-spouse beneficiaries who take a distribution from an inherited IRA rather than a direct trustee-to-trustee transfer permanently lose tax-deferred status on those funds, since inherited IRA rules allow no 60-day rollover for non-spouses. Savers are limited to one indirect (60-day) IRA-to-IRA rollover per 12-month period across all their IRAs, and a second attempt makes the extra distribution immediately taxable and potentially subject to a 10% early-withdrawal penalty if under 59½. Required minimum distributions (RMDs) must be fully satisfied before any Roth conversion in that year, and RMD dollars themselves cannot be used to fund a conversion — get the sequencing wrong and the error cannot be undone after the fact. These account-titling and distribution missteps have no IRS correction procedure, unlike simpler errors such as excess contributions, which can still be fixed if caught before the tax-filing deadline. _Sources: <https://www.fa-mag.com/news/7-fatal-errors--mistakes-that-cannot-be-fixed-88157.html>, <https://irahelp.com/who-pays-mistake-your-ira/>_

Bright MLS chief economist Lisa Sturtevant warned the 7% mark is also a "psychological barrier" that could further chill an already frozen housing market. _Sources: <https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/24/economy/mortgage-rate-tops-7-percent>, <https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/what-7-rates-could-mean-for-home-buyers>_
