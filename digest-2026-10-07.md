- **AI Solved One Math Problem and Everyone Freaked Out. It Just Cracked Hundreds More.** — I have enough concrete detail to write the brief now. Since October 2025, AI tools—most notably OpenAI's GPT-5 and successor models—have helped move roughly 100 of the late mathematician Paul Erdős's open problems into the "solved" column, according to a public tracker maintained by Fields Medalist Terence Tao; most of these resolutions amount to AI surfacing forgotten or obscure solutions already buried in the literature rather than genuine new proofs. The saga began when Columbia researchers Mehtaab Sawhney and Mark Sellke used ChatGPT to dig up solutions or partial solutions to roughly 20 Erdős problems, and escalated in mid-2026 when OpenAI said an internal model (and later an unreleased model called Astra) helped crack more significant, decades-old problems, including disproving the Erdős unit distance conjecture and resolving a problem open for 60 years in about 80 minutes. Tao has cautioned that most of these are "lowest-hanging fruit" solvable with existing techniques rather than profound mathematical breakthroughs, and that an earlier October 2025 claim that GPT-5 "solved" ten Erdős problems was walked back after it turned out the model had merely found pre-existing solutions, not made original discoveries. _Sources: <https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-uncovers-solutions-to-erdos-problems-moving-closer-to-transforming-math/>, <https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/openai-claims-it-solved-an-80-year-old-math-problem-for-real-this-time/>, <https://the-decoder.com/openais-gpt-5-4-pro-reportedly-solves-a-longstanding-open-erdos-math-problem-in-under-two-hours/>_

- **Surprise, Your Child Has a ‘Trump Account.’ Here’s What to Know.** — Trump Accounts, a federal savings program created by the "One Big Beautiful Bill" signed into law on July 4, 2025, launched for enrollment on July 4, 2026, giving every U.S. citizen child born between January 1, 2025, and December 31, 2028, a $1,000 seed deposit from the Treasury, invested in low-cost index funds and growing tax-deferred until age 18. As of early October 2026, the IRS reports about 4 million children have been signed up, with roughly 1 million families having claimed the $1,000 contribution, either online at trumpaccounts.gov or via IRS Form 4547 filed with a 2025 tax return. Treasury officials have also floated auto-enrolling eligible children by default, a change that could add as many as 60 million accounts. Parents or guardians can contribute up to $5,000 per year on top of the government seed money. _Sources: <https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/4-million-children-have-been-signed-up-for-trump-accounts-with-1-million-claiming-the-1000-pilot-program-contribution>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/trump-accounts-auto-enroll.html>_
