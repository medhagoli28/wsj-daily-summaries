- **OpenAI’s ChatGPT for Teens Poses ‘Unacceptable Risk,’ Nonprofit Says** — Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute released a report on Wednesday, October 7, 2026, rating OpenAI's ChatGPT for Teens an "Unacceptable Risk," arguing it should be restricted to users 18 and older until gaps are fixed. The nonprofit tested more than 4,000 prompts on accounts registered to 13- to 17-year-olds, both before and after OpenAI's August 18 rollout of the teen experience, and had the responses reviewed by child psychiatrists and a pediatrician. It found conversations involving suicidal ideation, self-harm, or disordered eating could run up to an hour without any parental notification, the chatbot missed more than one in four warranted crisis referrals, and the age-estimation system failed to reclassify adult accounts even when users said they were 13. OpenAI disputed the findings, saying the testing "does not accurately reflect how ChatGPT's teen safeguards work in practice." _Sources: <https://www.axios.com/2026/10/07/chatgpt-teens-safety-risk-common-sense-media>, <https://www.commonsensemedia.org/press-releases/chatgpt-for-teens-poses-unacceptable-risk-to-kids-common-sense-media-finds>, <https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/chatgpt-for-teens-keeps-teens-talking-even-during-mental-health-crises/>_

- **Five Things to Know About Tokenized Stocks** — Tokenized stocks are blockchain-based tokens, typically ERC-20 or SPL, that track the price of public shares like Apple or Tesla and are backed 1:1 by shares held with a regulated custodian, enabling 24/7 trading, fractional ownership, and near-instant settlement outside traditional exchanges like the DTCC. Kraken's xStocks, built by Backed Finance on Solana, launched May 22, 2025 with about 60 tickers but remains unavailable to US, UK, Canadian, and Australian users, while Robinhood switched on 200+ stock and ETF tokens for EU/EEA users on June 30, 2025 and has since expanded to over 2,000 tokens with a €1 minimum investment. On September 17, 2026, the SEC approved a temporary five-year "Innovation Exemption" letting permissioned Tokenized Securities Venues use automated market makers to trade tokenized NMS stocks in the US, provided token holders get the same dividend, voting, and liquidation rights as traditional shareholders. Public company issuers get 30 days to object if a third party tokenizes their stock without the company's initiation. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/17/sec-clears-path-for-tokenized-stocks-bringing-24/7-trading-closer.html>, <https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15083160-robinhood-tokenized-stocks-what-s-live-and-how-it-works>, <https://www.kraken.com/xstocks>_
- **Trump Accounts’ Extraordinary Expansion Puts Individual Stocks Into Kids’ Holdings** — The Treasury Department expanded the Trump Accounts program by automatically enrolling more than 60 million children, bringing the total to roughly 70 million kids with accounts, and issued temporary regulations effective September 30, 2026 allowing publicly traded companies to donate individual stock shares into the accounts, which must be held unsold for five years. - **European Stocks Should Withstand Bond Volatility** — Rising global bond yields have pressured European equities through September and into October 2026, with the STOXX Europe 600 falling 2.4% in euro terms in September and touching a more-than-three-month low on October 2 before rebounding 0.8% that Friday as oil prices eased and softer US jobs data cooled Fed rate-hike expectations. - **Exclusive | Wells Fargo Faces Regulator Probe Over Efforts to Boost Black Homeownership** — The U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development opened a fair-lending investigation into Wells Fargo on October 7, 2026, examining the bank's programs aimed at boosting Black homeownership, including a $60 billion lending commitment announced in 2017 to help at least 250,000 Black homeowners by 2027. HUD Secretary Scott Turner and Assistant Secretary Craig Trainor, in a letter to CEO Charlie Scharf, accused the bank of "sorting" homeowners by race and offering different mortgage products or terms based on race, potentially violating the Fair Housing Act; the probe also covers 2022 commitments of $210 million for racial equity in homeownership and $150 million to lower mortgage and refinancing costs. Wells Fargo had reportedly reached about 40% of its original $60 billion commitment and refinanced roughly 5,100 customers under a special-purpose credit program before commissioning a racial-equity assessment in late 2023. HUD said it is also reviewing similar initiatives at other U.S. banks. _Sources: <https://www.housingwire.com/articles/hud-investigation-wells-fargo-race/>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/real-estate/articles/wells-fargo-faces-hud-probe-171912286.html>, <https://www.mpamag.com/us/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/wells-fargo-faces-housing-probe-over-black-homeownership-lending/592708>_
