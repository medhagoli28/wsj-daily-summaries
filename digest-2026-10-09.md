- **Extreme Weather Is Everywhere Now—How to Prepare Your Home** — US homeowners insurance premiums are set to rise for a fifth straight year in 2026, with the average annual premium projected to increase about 4% to roughly $3,057, according to Insurance Journal, while rates have already climbed around 24-27% nationally between 2021 and 2024 as climate-driven storms, floods and wildfires intensify. - **SpaceX Makes Big Play to Become a Wireless Carrier** — SpaceX announced on October 8, 2026 that it agreed to buy a nationwide portfolio of low-band spectrum from investment firm Grain Management, covering as much as 14 megahertz of paired spectrum in the 800 MHz band, marking its most concrete step yet toward becoming a full-fledged U.S. wireless carrier.
