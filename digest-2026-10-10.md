- **The Desperate Hunt for AI Computing Power Is Upending Silicon Valley** — I have enough concrete, independently-confirmed detail to write the brief. The cost of renting Nvidia H100 chips on one-year contracts has jumped 60% over the past year, according to research firm SemiAnalysis, as demand for AI computing power outstrips supply and forces once-rival tech companies into unlikely partnerships. Cloud providers are now demanding multiyear contracts with upfront payments as high as 30%, sometimes backed by guarantors, squeezing startups while favoring hyperscalers, "neoclouds," and chipmakers who already control chips, data centers, and electricity. The scramble has pushed senior executives to personally negotiate chip and compute allocations, with examples including Elon Musk's xAI and Anthropic's Dario Amodei coordinating on computing resources, and Anthropic separately holding preliminary talks to lease roughly $10 billion worth of compute from Meta. Analysts warn the dynamic is raising concentration risk, as only the best-funded AI labs can lock in the capacity needed to keep training and running their models. _Sources: <https://investinglive.com/stocks/ai-compute-crunch-deepens-as-chip-rental-costs-jump-60-and-rivals-scramble-for-power/>, <https://explainx.ai/blog/amodei-asked-meta-wang-for-compute-wsj-claimed-vs-verified-2026>_
- **OpenAI Makes Progress in Preventing AI-Driven ChatGPT Delusions** — OpenAI said on October 14, 2026 that updates to GPT-5 cut "undesired" responses in challenging mental-health conversations by 39% compared with GPT-4o, after working with more than 170 mental-health experts across over 60 countries to retrain the model on recognizing and responding to psychosis, mania, suicidal intent, and unhealthy emotional reliance; the company also disclosed that roughly 0.07% of ChatGPT's active weekly users (about 560,000 people) show possible signs of psychosis or mania, and about 0.15% (1.2 million people) send messages indicating suicidal planning or intent. Independent researchers, including a study from Transluce, found newer models like GPT-5.6 were less likely than GPT-4o to reinforce delusional thinking, though OpenAI acknowledged its safeguards can still weaken during long conversations. CEO Sam Altman said on X this month that the company has "been able to mitigate the serious mental health issues" tied to the chatbot, as OpenAI faces more than a dozen lawsuits linking GPT-4o to user harm. _Sources: <https://openai.com/index/strengthening-chatgpt-responses-in-sensitive-conversations/>, <https://www.platformer.news/openai-mental-health-research-chatgpt-suicide-delusions/>, <https://www.eweek.com/news/openai-chatgpt-sensitive-responses/>_

## Personal Finance

- **Nvidia’s Subtle Change to How It Counts Cash Flow Has Big Implications for Buybacks** — Nvidia's September 28, 2026 announcement expanding its buyback authorization by $150 billion to a total of $235 billion included new language saying it will return value to shareholders through "excess free cash flow net of strategic uses," a subtle shift from its prior straightforward free-cash-flow framing. Analysts noted that under this broader lens — one that nets out Nvidia's outside equity investments and stock-based compensation costs — free cash flow for the first half of its fiscal year would fall from the officially reported $69.9 billion to roughly $21.7 billion, a nearly 70% drop. In that same six-month period, Nvidia spent about $35.2 billion on strategic equity investments, $4.5 billion on employee stock-related taxes, and $9 billion on actual buybacks, fueling investor questions about how much cash will realistically be available to execute the full $235 billion authorization. _Sources: <https://news.futunn.com/en/post/1000821066/nvidia-quietly-redefines-free-cash-flow-is-its-billion-dollar>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/nvidia-stock-buyback-investors.html>_
