- **Exclusive | Retirement Savers Sue Dodgers Owner Mark Walter Saying His Insurers Misled Them** — Los Angeles-area retiree Rick Phillips filed a lawsuit against Clear Spring Life and Annuity and Gainbridge on Monday (October 13, 2026), alleging the insurers—both tied to Dodgers chairman and CEO Mark Walter—misled annuity holders about how much of their money was funneled into affiliated, illiquid private-credit investments linked to Walter's Guggenheim Partners and TWG Global empire. It is at least the third such complaint, and plaintiffs also allege the insurers failed to disclose an ongoing federal investigation by the U.S. Attorney's Office in Manhattan and the SEC, stemming from a whistleblower complaint about misrepresented intercompany loans. - **Oil Climbs as Fighting Flares in Middle East, Ukraine** — Oil prices rose for a second straight session as Houthi forces intensified strikes on Saudi Arabia, including an attack on Riyadh's international airport, while two tankers were attacked in the Strait of Hormuz over the weekend, threatening a recovery in Middle East crude exports seen in September; Brent crude climbed to roughly $105.50 a barrel and WTI traded near $92-94 a barrel. Markets were also rattled by renewed Russian strikes on Ukrainian energy infrastructure, which cast doubt on a Friday "energy ceasefire" announcement and raised the risk that Kyiv would retaliate against Russian refineries. - **‘A Glorious Fate’ Review: Making a Union Martyr** — Historian Kevin M. Levin's biography "A Glorious Fate: The Life and Legacy of Colonel Robert Gould Shaw" was published by the University of North Carolina Press on September 29, 2026, tracing the Civil War officer's transformation from a privileged young man largely indifferent to abolitionism into the martyred commander of the 54th Massachusetts Infantry, one of the Union's first Black regiments. Shaw was killed on July 18, 1863, leading the regiment's assault on Confederate-held Fort Wagner in South Carolina, a battle that cemented his status as a Union hero and symbol of emancipation.
