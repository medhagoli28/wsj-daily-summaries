CSG Sets Sights on Space With Around $434 Million Stake in Canada’s MDA Space
Czechoslovak Group (CSG), the Czech defense and industrial conglomerate controlled by billionaire Michal Strnad, announced on October 13, 2026 that it had acquired a 9.5% equity stake in Canadian space technology firm MDA Space Ltd. for roughly C$618.5 million (about $434 million), making CSG the company's largest single shareholder. MDA Space, formerly MacDonald, Dettwiler and Associates and known for building the Canadarm and satellite robotics systems, has a market capitalization of around C$4.68 billion. Under the deal, CSG and MDA plan to explore joint co-investments, including developing satellite manufacturing and integration facilities in Europe and collaborating on future acquisitions in the space sector.
MARKETS
Publicis Account Wins Fuel Top-Line Growth
Publicis Groupe reported organic net revenue growth of 5% in the third quarter of 2026, up from 4.8% in the second quarter, beating analyst estimates of 4.8%, with net revenue reaching €3.81 billion ($4.4 billion) for the quarter and €11.04 billion for the nine-month period. The company credited a "record new-business run," including winning PepsiCo's global media account from Omnicom last month, with net new wins in 2026 expected to add roughly $465 million in revenue, or about 2 percentage points of organic growth, in 2027. Connected Media (62% of net revenue) posted high-single-digit organic growth, while Intelligent Creativity (25%) grew in the low single digits and the technology practice (13%) declined slightly, though its U.S. operations returned to growth. Publicis confirmed full-year 2026 guidance of 4.5%-5.0% organic net revenue growth, an operating margin slightly above 18.2%, and free cash flow of about €2.2 billion.
FINANCE
J&J Is Leaning Toward Spinning Out Its Hips-And-Knees Business
Johnson & Johnson is leaning toward spinning off its DePuy Synthes orthopedics unit—its hips, knees, and shoulder replacement business—into a standalone company rather than selling it, with CFO Joseph Wolk signaling the move on the company's October 14, 2026 earnings call; the division generated roughly $9.2 billion in revenue last year, about 10% of J&J's total sales, and a completed spinoff would create the largest pure-play orthopedics company. The decision follows J&J's 2023 spinoff of its consumer health unit Kenvue and reflects a strategy to concentrate on higher-growth, higher-margin areas like oncology, immunology, and cardiovascular care; a competing option—a roughly $20 billion sale of the unit to Apollo Global Management—had also been under discussion, but the spinoff path currently appears favored, with a target timeline around mid-2027. J&J also raised its full-year 2026 sales growth forecast to over 5% alongside the announcement.
FINANCE
European Indexes Largely Rise, Though AI Stocks Fall
The pan-European STOXX 600 index rose about 0.3-0.6% on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, recovering from the prior session's losses, with the broader gains driven by strong results from Dutch chip-equipment maker ASML, whose shares climbed roughly 1.8% after it raised its full-year sales outlook and said AI-driven demand for its lithography tools will outstrip supply through 2027-2028. Despite the index-level gains, European technology shares tied more directly to AI sentiment fell around 1% amid renewed concerns about the pace and sustainability of AI-related capital spending. STOXX 600 is up roughly 5.6% year-to-date, and strategists note that when the biggest AI-linked names are stripped out, European equities have actually outperformed their US counterparts in 2026.
FINANCE
Gold Futures Slip Ahead of U.S. CPI Data
Gold futures fell for a third straight session on October 14, 2026, with front-month Comex gold slipping about 0.3% to roughly $4,152.90 per ounce as traders trimmed positions ahead of the U.S. Consumer Price Index report due at 1230 GMT. Silver also declined, down about 1.6% to near $59.16 per ounce, as a firmer dollar and rising Treasury yields weighed on precious metals. Markets were focused on the inflation data for clues on the Federal Reserve's next move, with CME FedWatch data showing traders pricing in roughly an 88% chance of a December rate increase. The CPI release will be followed by Producer Price Index and retail sales data on Thursday, which traders expect could further swing gold prices depending on whether inflation comes in hotter or cooler than forecast.
