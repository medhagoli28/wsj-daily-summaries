Anthropic’s Quarterly Revenue Jumped 45% to Nearly $17 Billion
Anthropic told prospective investors that third-quarter revenue rose 45% to roughly $16.8 billion, up from about $11.6 billion in the second quarter and $4.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, according to an investor who attended a pre-IPO meeting with the company's leadership in San Francisco. The AI startup also said it was profitable on an adjusted basis in the quarter. The disclosures come as Anthropic prepares for a potential IPO targeting a valuation of over $2 trillion, though the offering is expected to be pushed until after the November 2026 U.S. midterm elections. The news follows Anthropic's $30 billion funding round earlier in 2026 that valued the company at $380 billion.

OpenAI Computing Chief to Depart
Sachin Katti, OpenAI's infrastructure lead and head of computing capacity, announced on October 14, 2026 that he is departing the role after about a year, with his last day set for November 15, 2026; he said on LinkedIn he "decided to move on to build something new" but will continue supporting OpenAI as an advisor. Katti joined OpenAI in late 2025 to oversee the company's Stargate data center buildout and helped secure computing agreements with providers including AWS. Uday Ruddarraju, OpenAI's CTO of compute, will take over and lead the company's overall compute organization. The departure comes as OpenAI has raised its computing-power spending forecast to roughly $750 billion by 2030.

GlobalFoundries to Build Two Chip Plants With Up to $21 Billion Funding
GlobalFoundries will build two new 300mm chip fabs in Malta, New York, backed by up to $21 billion in financing from Japan under a U.S.-Japan trade and investment agreement. The first facility is slated to come online in 2029, with roughly $10 billion earmarked to accelerate it and another $10 billion funding the second plant; each will have annual capacity of about 900,000 wafers, for a combined 1.8 million. As part of the deal the U.S. Department of Commerce will receive roughly 4% equity in GlobalFoundries, and the project is projected to create about 27,000 construction and planning jobs plus more than 1,000 manufacturing jobs.

Apple Closes Above the $5 Trillion Mark
Apple closed on October 14, 2026 at $342.96 a share, pushing its market capitalization above $5 trillion for the first time at market close, after the stock had briefly topped that level intraday six times before without holding it. The milestone makes Apple only the second company ever to close above $5 trillion, following Nvidia, which currently holds the top spot at roughly $5.33 trillion in market value. The close comes just over a month after John Ternus succeeded Tim Cook as Apple's CEO, and follows a brief stretch in July when Apple had overtaken Nvidia as the world's most valuable company before Nvidia reclaimed the lead.

Stocks Turn Lower as AI, Tech Shares Slump
The S&P 500 fell 1.1% to 7,718.94, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.9% to 26,692.87, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average slipped 0.1% to 51,621.48 on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, marking the S&P's biggest one-day drop since July 29. The decline was driven by hotter-than-expected September CPI data, which rose 3.7% year-over-year versus a 3.6% consensus, pushing Treasury yields higher and reviving rate-hike concerns. Malaysia Pharmacy Chain Braves Volatile Market With Mid-November Listing Plan
Big Caring Group Bhd, Malaysia's largest pharmacy operator formed by the 2023 merger of BIG Pharmacy and Caring Pharmacy, signed a retail underwriting agreement with Maybank Investment Bank, RHB Investment Bank and AmInvestment Bank in mid-October 2026 ahead of its planned Main Market listing on Bursa Malaysia. The IPO involves up to 1.9 billion shares (about 25.5% of enlarged share capital) — roughly 590.5 million new shares plus 1.3 billion existing shares sold by current holders — and could raise as much as RM3 billion ($750 million), one of Malaysia's largest listings in a decade, valuing the group at over $1.5 billion. The company, which operates 668 stores across brands including BIG Pharmacy, Caring Pharmacy and others, grew revenue at a 53.6% compound annual rate from RM1.1 billion in FY2023 to RM4 billion in FY2026. Big Caring is backed by private equity firm Creador and is targeting a listing around mid-November 2026.

Inflation Jump Will Push Social Security Benefits Up 3.6%
The Social Security Administration announced a 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment for 2027, up from 2.8% the prior year and the largest increase since 2023, driven by a jump in inflation data. The raise will boost the average retired worker's monthly benefit by about $75, to roughly $2,170, beginning with payments in January 2027; SSI recipients will see increased payments starting December 31, 2026. Nearly 72 million Social Security beneficiaries and about 7.3 million SSI recipients will be affected, with mailed notices going out starting in early December 2026 and online account updates available in late November.

The S&P 500 fell 1.11% to 7,718.94 on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.88% to 26,692.87, marking the S&P's first 1%-plus decline in 54 sessions after hotter-than-expected September CPI data revived fears of further Fed rate hikes. AI-adjacent and data-center "picks and shovels" stocks bore the brunt of the selloff: Dell slid 11.3%, Oracle fell 8.8%, Marvell dropped 8.7%, and CoreWeave tumbled 16%, with the group erasing roughly $80 billion in combined market value. FINANCE
Locking In a Mortgage Rate
The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate climbed to around 7.3%-7.4% in early-to-mid October 2026, up from 6.34% a year earlier and marking the highest levels since November 2023 after seven consecutive weeks of increases driven by a bond-market selloff and expectations of higher-for-longer rates. For buyers nearing a closing date, lenders typically offer rate locks of 30, 45, or 60 days that freeze the quoted rate regardless of market moves before closing, with extensions costing roughly 0.125%-0.25% of the loan amount if the lock expires first. Some lenders also sell float-down options, priced at about 0.25%-1% of the loan amount, that let a borrower who has locked a rate still capture a one-time reduction if market rates fall by a set threshold, often 0.25-0.5 percentage points, before closing. Given rates near a three-year high and climbing, financial advisers generally recommend locking promptly once a closing date is within 30-60 days rather than floating.
