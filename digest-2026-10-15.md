- **Locking In a Mortgage Rate** — The average 30-year fixed mortgage rate climbed to around 7.3%-7.4% in early-to-mid October 2026, up from 6.34% a year earlier and marking the highest levels since November 2023 after seven consecutive weeks of increases driven by a bond-market selloff and expectations of higher-for-longer rates. For buyers nearing a closing date, lenders typically offer rate locks of 30, 45, or 60 days that freeze the quoted rate regardless of market moves before closing, with extensions costing roughly 0.125%-0.25% of the loan amount if the lock expires first. Some lenders also sell float-down options, priced at about 0.25%-1% of the loan amount, that let a borrower who has locked a rate still capture a one-time reduction if market rates fall by a set threshold, often 0.25-0.5 percentage points, before closing. Given rates near a three-year high and climbing, financial advisers generally recommend locking promptly once a closing date is within 30-60 days rather than floating. _Sources: <https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/08/3377537/0/en/mortgage-rates-average-7-40.html>, <https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/30-year-mortgage-rate>, <https://www.forbes.com/advisor/mortgages/mortgage-rates-10-14-26/>_
