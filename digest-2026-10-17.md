- **AI Runs Into Its Next Big Obstacle: Insurance** — Insurers are increasingly unwilling to fully underwrite the risks of the AI boom, creating a growing coverage gap just as AI companies and data-center operators seek protection against massive potential losses. New ISO exclusion endorsements (CG 40 47 and CG 40 48) took effect January 1, 2026, letting insurers carve generative-AI-related bodily injury, property damage, and advertising-injury claims out of standard commercial general liability policies. AIG's CEO said in August 2026 that the AI data-center construction boom is "maxing out" property-and-casualty insurers' capacity, since reinsurers can only cover a fraction of the enormous construction-cost limits at these multibillion-dollar campuses. In response, specialty insurers have begun rolling out new products — such as Armilla's AI liability policy, underwritten by Lloyd's of London syndicates since April 2025, covering losses from AI hallucinations and model-performance failures — while analysts project the AI data-center insurance market could grow toward $200 billion by 2030. _Sources: <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/ai-data-center-boom-is-maxing-out-p-c-insurers-aig-ceo-says>, <https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/09/14/884900.htm>, <https://www.csis.org/analysis/insurance-industrys-retreat-ai-threatens-slow-innovation-and-adoption>_
