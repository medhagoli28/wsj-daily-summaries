- **Anthropic to Commit 5% of Its Shares to New ‘Public Benefit’ Fund** — Anthropic announced on October 18, 2026 that it will commit 5% of its outstanding shares to a newly created Public Benefit Fund, structured as a wholly-owned LLC, with board discretion to add another 2% for every 50% increase in the company's valuation above a set baseline, subject to stockholder approval. At Anthropic's last reported $965 billion valuation, the initial 5% stake would be worth roughly $48 billion, a figure that would rise to about $75 billion if the company reaches the roughly $1.5 trillion valuation some investors are reportedly discussing. The fund's charter bars spending on lobbying or political contributions and is intended to support charitable initiatives and efforts addressing AI's economic effects, including impacts on the labor market; the move comes weeks ahead of Anthropic's expected IPO. - **AI Agents Trading Stocks While You Sleep?
