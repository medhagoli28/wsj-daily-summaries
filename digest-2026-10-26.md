- **The Leak Suspicions That Got Three OpenAI Staffers Fired** — OpenAI fired three safety researchers — Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni, and Jasmine Wang — after an internal investigation suspected them of leaking confidential information to an outside AI-safety group, reportedly tied to a September report on the company's "Astra" model, which uses a design that makes its reasoning less visible to humans. ## Markets & Finance

- **Two AI Chipmakers Make Up 34% of America’s Earnings Boom** — Nvidia and Micron Technology together are projected to account for roughly a third of the S&P 500's third-quarter 2026 earnings growth, with FactSet data showing the two chipmakers contributing about 36% of the index's expected profit increase, according to estimates cited around October 13-21, 2026. The S&P 500's overall blended year-over-year earnings growth rate for Q3 2026 stands at about 29%, but excluding Nvidia and Micron, that growth rate would fall to roughly 20%—meaning the pair added close to 9 percentage points on their own. Nvidia has benefited from surging AI chip demand while Micron has seen a boost from memory-chip pricing tied to AI infrastructure buildout. The concentration underscores how reliant the broader market's earnings expansion has become on a small handful of AI-linked semiconductor companies rather than broad-based corporate growth. _Sources: <https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/21/micron-and-nvidia-are-the-2-biggest-drivers-of-s-and-p-500-earnings-growth-are-their-stocks-buys/>, <https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9121840/micron-technology-mu-and-nvidia-drive-36-of-sp-500-q3-earnings-growth>, <https://finance.biggo.com/news/3909af29-5d10-4489-a0fe-bf5ba9716c35>_

- **How Trade Desk Rescued Executives’ Stock Options From a Dive in the Share Price** — Trade Desk shareholders approved a plan to reprice up to roughly 14.7 million underwater stock options at a special meeting in October 2026, after the ad-tech company's shares had fallen about 77% over the prior year, pushing most outstanding option exercise prices above the current stock price. The repricing reset about 1.6 million options for CEO Jeff Green and another 1.6 million for other senior executives to that day's closing price, with more than 12 million additional options for other employees also converted from deeply underwater to in the money. The measure passed by nearly a two-to-one margin only because Green's supervoting shares, which give him just over 50% of voting power, accounted for roughly 98% of the "yes" votes; excluding his stake, about 95% of outside shareholders voted against it. The company justified the move by saying declines in its stock price "could substantially harm" its ability to retain employees holding the options. _Sources: <https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/trade-desk-ceo-overrides-investor-opposition-to-option-repricing--wsj-4970999>, <https://www.boardroomalpha.com/research/trade-desk-option-repricing-vote-2026>, <https://www.adweek.com/media/trade-desk-stock-slide-employee-retention-reprice-options/>_

- **Meta’s Hunt for AI Capital Strikes a Raw Nerve on Wall Street** — Meta priced a record $30 billion corporate bond sale on October 30, 2025, split into six tranches maturing from five to 40 years, to help fund its AI data-center buildout, with Citigroup and Morgan Stanley leading the deal; it ranks among the largest high-grade corporate bond offerings ever and the biggest since 2023.
