## Tech

- **Banks, Blackstone Form $63 Billion Financing Vehicle for Broadcom AI Tech** — Bank of America, Citigroup, Morgan Stanley and Blackstone have formed a $63 billion special-purpose vehicle to finance Broadcom AI chips and networking gear that will be leased to Anthropic, marking one of the largest debt deals yet tied to the AI infrastructure buildout. The deal is structured with roughly $43.5 billion (about 69%) as first-lien senior debt arranged by Bank of America, Citigroup and Morgan Stanley, while Blackstone is committing the second-lien portion; Broadcom is providing residual value support on the first-lien tranche. The financing ties back to a previously announced strategic compute agreement between Broadcom and Anthropic, with chip deliveries expected to begin in 2027. _Sources: <https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/>, <https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-banks-blackstone-line-up-63-billion-to-finance-broadcom-chips-for-anthropic-4973662>, <https://www.streetinsider.com/Corporate+News/Four+banks+form+$63B+financing+vehicle+for+Broadcom+AI+chips+leased+to+Anthropic/27234241.html>_
- **The Biggest Takeaways From Anthropic’s IPO Prospectus** — Anthropic's S-1 filed with the SEC shows first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion, up roughly 13-fold from $1.16 billion in the same period of 2025, with Q2 revenue growing 1,368% year-over-year and net revenue retention near 900%. The filing also discloses an $80.7 billion net loss for H1 2026, but $72.4 billion of that stems from a non-cash accounting charge tied to convertible notes, leaving the company adjusted-profitable with roughly $1.6 billion in operating income; compute costs per dollar of revenue fell from $2.01 a year earlier to $0.55. Anthropic plans to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT" the week of November 16, 2026, targeting a valuation of $1.8-$2 trillion, more than double its $965 billion valuation from a May funding round, while its seven co-founders retain voting control and nearly 25% of revenue comes from just five customers. _Sources: <https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/10/26/anthropic-just-filed-its-ipo-prospectus-here-s-what-investors-learned/>, <https://www.wionews.com/world/anthropic-s1-filing-ipo-loss-cloud-computing-commitments-1793022200164>, <https://www.inc.com/chris-morris/anthropic-just-filed-for-a-historic-ipo-revealing-an-80-billion-loss-in-the-first-half-of-the-year/91380570>_
- **Anthropic Unveils Paperwork for Blockbuster IPO** — Anthropic publicly filed its Form S-1 with the SEC on October 26, 2026, setting up a Nasdaq listing under the ticker "ANT" with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the underwriting syndicate. The filing disclosed revenue of $16.3 billion for the first half of 2026, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, alongside a net loss of $80.7 billion, most of which stemmed from a $72.4 billion non-cash charge tied to changes in the fair value of convertible notes. Anthropic is targeting a listing the week of November 16, with bankers discussing a valuation range of roughly $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion and a raise that could exceed $100 billion, positioning it to rival or surpass SpaceX's IPO as one of the largest public debuts in history. The filing also included extensive risk disclosures warning that its AI technology could pose "catastrophic or existential" risks. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html>, <https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/anthropic-releases-ipo-filing-ahead-highly-anticipated-stock-debut-rcna605676>, <https://www.npr.org/2026/10/26/nx-s1-6014772/anthropic-ai-ipo-could-break-records>_
- **Meta Plans up to $95 Billion in Financing to Power Its AI Growth** — Meta announced on October 26, 2026, a plan to raise up to $95 billion through equity offerings to fund its AI infrastructure buildout, including data centers and cloud computing capacity. The plan combines an initial $35 billion tranche—via concurrent underwritten public offerings of depositary shares and Class A common stock tied to forward sale agreements—with a separate $60 billion share-sale program using nonvoting Class C shares. Meta said chipmakers Nvidia, AMD, and Broadcom expressed interest in purchasing up to $5 billion of the shares. The move supports Meta's projected 2026 capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion, driven by investment in AI compute, its Muse AI agent, and data center expansion. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/meta-stock-offering-ai.html>, <https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/meta-targets-up-to-usd-95-billion-in-capital-offerings-to-scale-ai-compute>_

## Markets & Finance

- **Brazilian Markets Crash After Lula’s Surprise Election Win Stuns Investors** — Lula da Silva won an unprecedented fourth term in Brazil's October 26, 2026 runoff, defeating Senator Flávio Bolsonaro with 51.8% of valid votes to 48.2% in a comeback after losing the first round — the first time since Brazil's return to direct elections in 1989 that a runoff winner had trailed in round one. The surprise outcome, which blindsided investors who had bet on a Bolsonaro win and fiscal tightening, triggered a historic market rout: Brazil's Bovespa index fell nearly 17% (triggering a trading-halt circuit breaker), the real dropped more than 10% against the dollar, and the iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZ) sank about 25% in premarket trading. Individual stocks were hit even harder, with Banco Bradesco down 31.5%, Itaú Unibanco down 26.6%, XP Inc. down 48.7%, and StoneCo down 37.0%, while roughly $6 billion in planned corporate equity sales were derailed; Bolsonaro refused to concede and said he would seek to annul the result, adding to the uncertainty. _Sources: <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-26/brazil-markets-brace-for-rout-on-lula-s-improbable-election-win>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/brazil-lula-win-presidential-election-runoff-vote.html>, <https://www.semafor.com/article/10/26/2026/brazilian-markets-plunge-after-lulas-surprise-election-victory>_
