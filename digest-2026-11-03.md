## Tech

- **Silicon Valley Promises AI Will Cure Disease. Drugmaker CEOs Aren’t Buying It.** — Pharmaceutical executives are pushing back on Silicon Valley's bold predictions that AI will soon cure most diseases, with Jeff Leiden, executive chairman of Vertex Pharmaceuticals, calling a tech leader's claim that AI would cure 90% of human disease within seven years "complete BS"; Vertex says it worked with roughly 50 AI companies to mine a dataset of 10,000 drug compounds and produced zero working drugs. AstraZeneca CEO Pascal Soriot echoed the skepticism, noting "disease is complicated, biology is complicated," and executives point out that no FDA-approved drug has yet been designed using AI, with about a quarter of mid-to-late-stage drug failures tied to "off-target" safety issues that current AI models struggle to predict. The pushback comes after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei suggested in September 2026 that AI could help cure most human disease within five to ten years. Still, major drugmakers including AstraZeneca, Bayer, Eli Lilly, and Regeneron continue to use AI for tasks like mining patient records and identifying drug targets. _Sources: <https://www.benzinga.com/news/health-care/26/11/62860238/drugmaker-ceos-say-ai-alone-cant-cure-diseases-challenge-silicon-valleys-bold-predictions-to-say-ai-is-going-to-cure-cancer>, <https://www.lifesciencefocus.com/article/view/1254937/pharmaceutical_executives_play_down_ai_hype_see_no_quick_path_to_cures>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/drugmaker-ceos-ai-alone-cant-153014399.html>_
- **Google Curbs AI in School Accounts After Pushback** — Google turned off Gemini, Gemini in Classroom, and AI-infused Search by default for K-12 student accounts managed through Google Workspace for Education, announcing the reversal on November 3, 2026, after backlash from parents, teachers, and privacy advocates over AI being enabled without consent. School administrators must now actively opt in to restore any of these tools, and the rollout of the changed defaults begins in early 2027. Notably, Google still bundles AI features into Search itself — a district can disable the Gemini chatbot while keeping Search, but it cannot keep Search while stripping out its AI Overviews, since there is no separate toggle for that. Google also said data from these school accounts will be used only to generate responses to a user's prompts and not to train or improve its generative AI models. _Sources: <https://qz.com/google-schools-restrict-ai-lose-search-110326>, <https://en.sedaily.com/world/2026/11/08/google-blocks-gemini-by-default-for-us-school-accounts>, <https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330004/20261104/google-requires-ai-features-schools-risk-losing-search-access-guided-learning-rollout.htm>_

- **Comprehensive Financial Planning: What Is It, and How Does It Work?** — This is an evergreen explainer piece (part of WSJ Buy Side's personal-finance guide series) rather than a breaking news story, so no competing outlet reported the same article. Comprehensive financial planning is generally described as a holistic approach coordinating six core areas — cash flow/budgeting, protection (insurance), tax planning, investment planning, retirement planning, and estate planning — into one integrated strategy rather than addressing goals like retirement or college savings in isolation. The process itself follows a standard sequence: gathering financial facts, defining goals, modeling options, implementing a plan, and revisiting it periodically as circumstances change. Advisors who offer this service are typically recommended to hold fiduciary status and credentials such as CFP certification, since the role involves managing risk, tax efficiency, and life changes across a client's full financial picture rather than a single product or account.
