## Tech

- **How the Data Center Backlash Could Play Into the Hands of Big Tech** — Voters in at least 38 jurisdictions passed bans or restrictions on new data centers on Election Day in early November 2026, part of a broader backlash driven by concerns over noise, water use, and higher electricity bills — a Washington Post/other polling found roughly 7 in 10 Americans oppose new data centers in their communities, and roughly $64 billion in data center projects have been blocked or delayed over the past two years. Analysts and reporting (Axios, TechCrunch) note this regulatory friction raises the barrier to entry for new projects while established players like Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Meta — which already have sites permitted, built, or under construction — are better positioned to weather the pushback and even acquire stalled projects cheaply, effectively entrenching their competitive advantage. In response, companies including Google, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft have begun offering communities more money, transparency, and concessions to ease opposition, according to Fortune and Axios. _Sources: <https://www.axios.com/2026/11/05/ai-data-center-election-backlash-concessions>, <https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/04/after-the-midterms-a-bigger-clash-over-data-centers-could-be-coming/>, <https://fortune.com/2026/10/22/google-meta-amazon-microsoft-data-center-backlash/>_
- **HP Taps Former GSK Executive Emma Walmsley as Next CEO** — HP Inc.'s board named Emma Walmsley as its next chief executive, effective January 4, 2027, succeeding Bruce Broussard, who has been interim CEO since February 2026; the announcement came November 4, 2026, ending a roughly monthlong search. Walmsley led pharmaceutical giant GSK as CEO from April 2017 to December 2025 and has sat on Microsoft's board for seven years, but she arrives at HP with no prior hardware or tech-industry experience. Her mandate includes reviving a sluggish PC business and positioning HP to capture growing demand for AI-enabled devices. _Sources: <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/hp-names-emma-walmsley-as-new-ceo-ending-monthlong-search>, <https://kfgo.com/2026/11/04/hp-names-former-gsk-chief-emma-walmsley-as-ceo/>_

Harley-Davidson, by contrast, posted a 9% jump in North American retail motorcycle sales in its third quarter of 2026 (reported October 29, 2026), marking a fourth consecutive quarter of year-over-year growth, and the company raised its full-year 2026 guidance to global retail sales and wholesale shipments of 135,500 to 139,000 units. Analysts attribute the boat and RV slump to higher financing costs, inflation, and more cautious consumer confidence around big-ticket discretionary purchases, even as Harley's turnaround—driven by strength in its Trike and Softail lines—bucks that broader pullback. _Sources: <https://powersportsbusiness.com/news/harley-davidson/2026/11/03/harley-davidson-q3-2026-retail-sales-dealer-inventory/>, <https://rvbusiness.com/soft-market-continues-for-the-recreational-boating-industry/>, <https://globalviewsworld.com/boating-industry-faces-15-sales-drop-in-2026/>_
- **A Soldier Cut Off His Own Foot to Survive. Then Came a Daring Drone Rescue.** — Ukrainian soldier Kostiantyn Vlasenko, 49, amputated his own frostbitten and gangrenous foot to survive after being wounded by Russian drone-dropped munitions that killed his comrade, then spent roughly 491 days stranded near the front line before a six-rotor heavy-lift drone extracted him on October 4, 2026, carrying him about 70 meters into the air and 15 kilometers to safety. ## Personal Finance

- **The 2027 Tax Brackets Are Here. See Where You Land.** — The IRS announced new 2027 federal income tax brackets on November 10, 2026, keeping the seven rates unchanged at 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, and 37% while raising the income thresholds roughly 3.2% for inflation, pushing the top 37% bracket to kick in above $768,700 for single filers. The standard deduction rises to $16,600 for single filers, $33,200 for married couples filing jointly, and $24,925 for heads of household. The adjustments touch nearly 70 provisions, including an increase in the unified gift and estate tax exemption to $15.48 million and a rise in the annual gift tax exclusion to $20,000 per recipient. These changes apply to tax year 2027, meaning they'll affect returns filed in early 2028. _Sources: <https://thehill.com/business/6211112-irs-new-tax-brackets-standard-deductions-2027/>, <https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/irs-releases-2027-tax-brackets-222145974.html>_
