- **How Hackers Steal Millions in AI Computing Power** — I now have solid, non-WSJ confirmation of this story with concrete figures and dates. Hackers have been breaking into cloud-hosting and AI developer platforms to steal API keys and credentials, then running up massive unauthorized usage charges on victims' accounts — a technique security researchers call "LLMjacking." In one case, attackers exploited a previously unknown vulnerability in Railway's build infrastructure starting October 20, 2026, eventually decrypting customer environment variables across up to 100,000 workspaces and racking up an estimated $740,000 in fraudulent AI usage charges that Railway had to absorb. The Railway incident was part of a wider wave — dubbed "Black October" by researchers — in which at least a dozen AI developer, hosting, and inference platforms (including Zeabur, OpenCode, Northflank, Fireworks AI, and NVIDIA Brev) were breached between early October and early November 2026, with stolen keys often validated and exploited within minutes; in some cases two stolen Amazon Bedrock keys alone generated roughly 800,000 and 1.7 million model calls. Researchers also describe an emerging underground resale market, with "LLM Mixers" pooling stolen keys and selling access to over 30 LLM providers. _Sources: <https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/railway-says-stolen-customer-ai-keys.html>, <https://www.csoonline.com/article/4247578/hackers-stole-ai-credentials-to-get-740000-in-free-compute-railway-picked-up-the-bill.html>, <https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-11-02-ai-developer-platforms-breaches-northflank-wiz/>_

## Markets & Finance

- **How Boise Became One of America’s Hottest Rental Markets** — Boise, Idaho has emerged as one of the fastest-rising rental markets in the country, with average apartment rent reaching $1,585 in November 2026, up roughly 8.1% year-over-year, while Apartment List pegs the annual increase even higher at around 16% over the first nine months of 2026, far outpacing the 4.1% Idaho state average and the roughly flat-to-negative national rate. Two-bedroom units in the city now average $1,690, up 17% from a year earlier, and one-bedrooms average $1,450, up about 11.5%. Among the nation's 100 largest cities, only San Francisco and Oakland posted faster rent growth than Boise over the past year, a reversal from the slower 4.8% pace Boise saw in the same period of 2025. _Sources: <https://boisedev.com/news/2026/09/15/boise-apartment-rents-are-near-record-levels-again-only-two-cities-grew-faster-last-month/>, <https://www.apartmentlist.com/rent-report/id/boise>, <https://idahonews.com/news/local/boise-rents-keep-climbing-2-bedroom-units-increase-17-percent-to-1690>_

- **Toyota’s Scion Brand Returns With Japan’s Forbidden-Fruit Minitrucks** — Toyota is reviving its Scion brand in late 2027 with the KEI-01, a U.S.-legal kei-style minitruck built on the classic Japanese formula of a 660cc three-cylinder engine, four-wheel drive, and a compact body roughly 11 feet long; it will be assembled at Toyota's Indiana plant, where the company plans to invest $34 million and add 50 jobs, with the truck imported partly as a knockdown kit from Japan to meet Low-Speed Vehicle rules (25-mph top speed) and FMVSS regulation 500. - **Maybelline Redefines ‘Maybe’ in a Refresh of Its Classic Catchphrase** — Maybelline New York has retired its 35-year-old slogan "Maybe she's born with it. Maybe it's Maybelline" (introduced in 1991) for a new version, "Maybe. We're born with it. Maybe. It's Maybelline," swapping "she's" for "we're" and adding periods to recast "maybe" as a declaration rather than a question. The brand unveiled the refreshed tagline around November 11-16, 2026, alongside a new campaign called "The Beauty of Infinite Possibility" fronted by global ambassador Miley Cyrus. Maybelline says the change extends the line's meaning from one woman to all women, framing "beauty" as something open to interpretation and possibility rather than a fixed trait. Reaction has been mixed, with some outlets like Forbes calling the new phrasing "polarizing" compared with the "iconic" original. _Sources: <https://www.forbes.com/sites/rachelburchfield/2026/11/11/maybelline-rolls-out-polarizing-new-tagline-to-replace-maybe-shes-born-with-it-maybe-its-maybelline-slogan-after-35-years/>, <https://www.marketingdive.com/news/maybelline-updates-maybe-tagline-as-beauty-perceptions-evolve/874376/>, <https://wwd.com/beauty-industry-news/beauty-features/miley-cyrus-maybe-its-maybelline-new-slogan-1239402476/>_
- **A 55-Year-Old Film That Offended Everyone Is Back—and This Time It’s a Hit** — Ken Russell's 1971 film "The Devils," long banned, cut, and censored for its graphic depictions of sex, violence, and religious blasphemy, returned to U.S. theaters on October 16, 2026, via Warner Bros.' new Clockwork imprint in a fully restored, uncut 4K director's cut — the first NC-17 release in Warner Bros.' 103-year history. - **‘Avedon’ Review: Netflix’s Documentary on a Defining Portraitist** — "Avedon," directed by Ron Howard, premiered on Netflix in November 2026 as a documentary profile of photographer Richard Avedon, drawing on previously unseen archival material and interviews with friends, family, and peers to trace his career turning head-on portraiture into a recognized art form. The film has been met with widespread critical acclaim, holding a Metascore of 82 ("universal acclaim") based on nine critic reviews aggregated by Metacritic as of mid-November 2026.
