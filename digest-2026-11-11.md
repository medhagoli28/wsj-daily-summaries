- **Exclusive | United Airlines Is Banning AI Bots From Booking Flights** — United Airlines updated its contract of carriage on November 11, 2026, to bar unauthorized third-party AI agents from booking flights, managing reservations, or accessing MileagePlus loyalty accounts on customers' behalf, citing risks to customer data, surprise purchases, and unauthorized changes. ## Markets & Finance

- **Why High Beef Prices Are Here to Stay** — Ground beef hit a record retail average of $6.92 per pound in August 2026, while beef steak averaged $12.80 per pound, up 16% from a year earlier, according to CNBC. The root cause is supply: USDA's January 2026 inventory count put the total U.S. cattle and calf herd at 86.2 million head and the beef cow herd at 27.6 million, the smallest national herd in 75 years and the lowest cow count since 1961, after years of drought and high feed costs drove ranchers to liquidate rather than rebuild. USDA projects retail beef prices will climb roughly 10% in 2026, and because cattle need two to three years to breed, raise, and bring to market, analysts say the herd can't be rebuilt quickly, so elevated prices are expected to persist for several more years. Bloomberg and Fortune both note demand hasn't meaningfully softened despite the run-up, which is keeping prices locked in at or near record levels. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/15/beef-cattle-grilling-inflation.html>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/02/beef-prices-demand-for-steak-isnt-falling.html>, <https://fortune.com/2026/04/22/beef-prices-record-high-wont-come-down-2028/>_
- **The Global Economy Looks Vulnerable to an AI Bust** — IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva warned on November 16, 2026 that the global economy is "standing on one leg, the AI leg," citing roughly $1 trillion in AI-related circular financing, about two-thirds of it debt-funded, as a financial-stability risk if confidence in the sector falters. The IMF's October 2026 outlook raised its global growth forecast to 3.1% largely on the back of AI infrastructure investment, but warned that a sharp AI investment pullback could cut global GDP by 1.1% by 2028 (including a 1.8% hit to US output), with even a moderate equity correction shaving about 0.4 percentage points off growth. Separately, a November Bank of America survey found 45% of fund managers now name an "AI bubble" as the market's top tail risk, up from just 11% in September, while former SEC Chair Gary Gensler has warned a bubble burst could trigger a recession comparable in scale to the dot-com bust. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/imf-georgieva-warning-ai-circular-financing.html>, <https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-10-12/imf-says-ai-investment-propping-up-global-growth-but-war-and-debt-threaten-outlook>, <https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/11/63288196/global-economy-is-standing-on-one-leg-as-ai-boom-raises-1-trillion-financial-stability-risks-imf-chief-warns-we-have-to-be-watchful>_

- **CoreWeave Finds a Workaround to Capital Constraints—for Now** — CoreWeave reported a Q3 2026 net loss of about $709 million even as revenue surged roughly 168% year-over-year, prompting the company to overhaul how it funds its AI data-center buildout.
