- **Our New Cybersecurity Reality: If It’s ‘Smart,’ It’s Hackable** — Attackers seized remote control of Danfoss refrigeration controllers in hundreds of French supermarkets overnight on October 11-12, 2026, forcing units into continuous defrost mode that pushed internal temperatures as high as 50°C (122°F) and spoiled fresh and frozen stock at chains including Intermarché, Netto, Carrefour, and Monoprix. - **Exclusive | AI Bots Are Using Credit Cards. Banks Are Racing to Keep Up.** — JPMorgan Chase has begun blocking AI shopping agents from completing purchases with customers' Chase credit card numbers, even when the customer has handed the agent the card details, while Bank of America, Capital One and Citigroup cards are already being used by such agents when customers permit it; JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon warned on the bank's October 2026 third-quarter earnings call against letting "rogue agents" — "there could be thousands of them" — into its systems. The dispute centers partly on card classification, since the virtual cards many AI agents use get coded as commercial cards, prompting workarounds like Stripe's Link wallet, which issues one-time virtual cards that merchants say cost more to accept and complicate returns. Meanwhile card networks are racing to build rails for this activity: Mastercard introduced "Verifiable Intent" on March 5, 2026, and Visa launched "Intelligent Commerce Connect" on April 8, 2026, with Visa and OpenAI announcing on June 10, 2026 a deal letting ChatGPT agents pay with linked Visa cards within user-set limits.
