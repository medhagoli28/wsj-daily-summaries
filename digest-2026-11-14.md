## Personal Finance

- **What Travelers Caught in the Air-Travel Delays Need to Know** — A nationwide FAA ground stop triggered by a telecommunications outage halted U.S. air traffic for roughly four and a half hours on Friday, November 13, 2026, from about 3:30 p.m. to 8:15 p.m. ET, leading to roughly 12,462 flight cancellations and thousands more delays, with major airlines alone canceling about 9,082 flights. Under Department of Transportation rules, travelers whose flights were canceled or significantly changed are entitled to a full cash refund "regardless of the reason," including an FAA-ordered stoppage, but only if they decline any rebooking offer and any travel voucher or credit the airline proposes instead. Airlines must process these refunds automatically and promptly, typically within about a week for credit-card purchases, without requiring the passenger to request them. Unlike in Europe, U.S. carriers are not required to pay additional cash compensation or cover meals and lodging for stranded passengers even when the disruption is attributable to the airline. _Sources: <https://www.washingtonpost.com/transportation/2026/11/13/faa-grounds-flights-all-us-airports-citing-communications-issue/>, <https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa>, <https://www.kqed.org/news/12109790/faa-ground-stop-plane-delays-cancelations-refund-compensation-your-rights>_
