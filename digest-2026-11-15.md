## Tech

- **Jensen Huang’s Downfall** — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned on Thursday, November 12, 2026, after 33 years leading the company, following reports that he had extramarital affairs — including one with a woman U.S. intelligence reportedly believes was a spy for China — and the board moved to oust him just a day after The Wall Street Journal first reported it was preparing to do so. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO while Nvidia's board searches for a permanent successor, considering both internal and external candidates. Nvidia shares had already lost more than $300 billion in market value in after-hours trading on November 11 once the WSJ report broke, with losses reportedly growing toward roughly $1 trillion in the following days as the scandal widened and raised fresh governance concerns for the roughly $4 trillion company Huang co-founded in 1993. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html>, <https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/12/tech/jensen-huang-nvidia-resigns>, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-11/nvidia-ceo-had-an-affair-with-health-executive-at-firm-nyt-says>_
- **How Jensen Huang Built the Backdoor Route That Keeps Beijing in the AI Race** — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang cultivated a network of Southeast Asian partners, centered on Singapore-based cloud provider Megaspeed and its Malaysian subsidiary Speedmatrix, that moved roughly $7.5 billion worth of restricted Nvidia AI chips to Chinese firms, with Speedmatrix alone receiving over 200 shipments of the technology between June 2024 and June 2025. The scandal escalated after reports surfaced that Huang had a personal relationship with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, a Chinese citizen described in reporting as a "publicly reported Chinese intelligence asset," prompting Nvidia's board to open an independent national-security investigation and, as of November 2026, consider removing him as CEO. Separately, Malaysian customs seized about $12.1 million worth of advanced AI chips in October 2025, and Singapore's government has continued discussions with U.S. officials, including Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau, over the Megaspeed probe. Chinese recipients of the rerouted computing capacity reportedly included major firms such as Alibaba and Tencent. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/megaspeed-nvidia-jensen-huang-alice-huang.html>, <https://fortune.com/2026/11/11/megaspeed-nvidia-chips-china-singapore-malaysia/>_

Bessent gave Nvidia's board and outside legal counsel 10 days to provide answers. Nvidia has named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO and Harvey C. - **How AI Helps Me Find the Right TV Shows at the Right Times** — Netflix began testing a new "AI Discover" tab on November 3, 2026, giving a limited group of U.S. subscribers who use Netflix in English on supported TVs a mood- and time-based way to pick what to watch. Instead of a search box, the feature offers curated themes such as "Something Light," "Scare Me," "Family Movie Night," and "Quick Watches," each previewed with a short clip, and every recommended title carries a "Tune Your Recommendations" button next to "Play" and "My List" so users can keep or reject it. Netflix described the rollout as a "test kitchen," saying it will use subscriber feedback to decide which discovery features to keep, improve, or expand. The company has not given a timeline for a wider rollout beyond the initial test group. _Sources: <https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/netflixs-new-ai-discover-tab-helps-you-find-something-to-watch-based-on-your-mood/>, <https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/netflix-launches-ai-discover-content-recommendations-1236937769/>_

Anthropic is marketing shares at an estimated $70-$78 each, targeting a valuation of around $1.3 trillion to $1.5 trillion for the IPO — down from talk of over $2 trillion just weeks earlier — after its most recent private round (Series H, May 2026) valued the company at $965 billion. Seven major tech companies have already committed roughly $26 billion toward the offering, and analysts have noted Bitcoin fell about 18% in the weeks ahead of Anthropic's IPO, echoing a similar ~19% drop before SpaceX's listing, a pattern some attribute to investors freeing up cash to chase the new shares. _Sources: <https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-ipo-reportedly-oversubscribed-7-035659320.html>, <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html>, <https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-targets-1-3-trillion-valuation-in-ipo/>_

- **How Jensen Huang Built a Business to Feed Chinese Appetite for American Chips** — Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO, president, and board member on November 11, 2026, after an internal probe into his relationship with Alice Huang, a Chinese businesswoman and then-CEO of Singapore-based Megaspeed International, whose network of shell companies allegedly routed roughly 240,000 Nvidia GPUs — including H100 and H200 chips — to Chinese buyers in violation of U.S. export restrictions, worth an estimated $7.5 billion. The move followed hotter-than-expected U.S. inflation data and hawkish commentary from Federal Reserve officials, which pushed market pricing to nearly fully price in a Fed rate hike at the December meeting, with some analysts (including RBC Capital Markets) flagging a real chance of back-to-back hikes in December and January. Louis Vuitton Is Reluctant to Follow the Trend.** — Gucci has cut prices on select handbags by roughly 29-30%, including a France-sold bag that dropped from €3,500 to €2,450 and the Ophidia mini bucket bag in China that fell from 12,300 yuan to 8,700 yuan, moves that followed 13 consecutive quarters of declining sales at the Kering-owned brand. - **The Morning Risk Report: Jensen Huang Doesn’t Believe in Succession Planning. It’s Become a Big Risk for Nvidia.** — Jensen Huang, 63, has publicly rejected formal succession planning at Nvidia, telling podcaster Lex Fridman earlier in 2026 that he hopes to "die at his desk," even as the company's market value has approached $4 trillion with no named president, COO, or clear No. 2 executive; Huang reportedly has roughly 60 direct reports in a deliberately flat management structure. ## Personal Finance

- **Sean Duffy Made the Call to Shut Down Flights. Now He’s Making His Case.** — Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy ordered a nationwide ground stop on Friday, November 13, 2026, halting departures for roughly four hours before airspace reopened that evening, in what became the longest U.S. flight-departure halt since 9/11; nearly 18,000 flights were canceled from Friday through Monday, November 16. Duffy attributed the breakdown to a contractor-installed router in Arkansas that bombarded air traffic control's telecommunications network with repeated connection attempts, overwhelming systems that feed radar data to controllers, though some of the roughly 13,000 air traffic controllers working unpaid during the government shutdown publicly disputed his account. Duffy has since pressed Congress for $50 billion in emergency funding to modernize the aging air traffic control system, up from an earlier $30 billion ask, while some Democrats note much of the money Congress already allocated for FAA upgrades remains unspent. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/15/flight-delays-ground-faa-stop.html>, <https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/15/politics/live-news/trump-administration-ground-stop>, <https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/duffy-sounds-alarm-over-aging-faa-system-after-nationwide-air-travel-shutdown>_
