- **Temu Gets Fresh EU Warning Over Efforts to Curb Sale of Illegal Products** — On November 17, 2026, the European Commission issued preliminary findings that Temu breached the EU's Digital Services Act by failing to adequately prevent the sale of illegal and unsafe products, citing ineffective product-compliance checks, weak content moderation, and a lax sanctions system that let traders commit repeated serious infringements before being suspended. - **‘Protestants and Patriots’ Review: Good and Godly Resistance** — D. G. Hart, a history professor at Hillsdale College, published "Protestants and Patriots: Presbyterians in the Age of Revolution" through the University of Notre Dame Press in April 2026. The book traces Presbyterianism's transatlantic political history from roughly 1560 to 1870, covering England, Scotland, Ireland, the United States, and Canada, with a focus on the 1630s-40s religious and parliamentary wars in Britain—about 150 years before the American Revolution—as the movement's most disruptive period. - **It’s Hard to Protect Your Nest Egg from Nvidia** — I now have solid, concrete numbers from legitimate outlets (CNBC, Motley Fool) to write the brief.
