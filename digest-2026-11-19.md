- **These Charts Show How More People Are Pulling Cash Out of Their Homes** — Homeowners withdrew about $47 billion in home equity during the first quarter of 2026, up 2% from a year earlier and the highest first-quarter total since 2021, according to ICE Mortgage Technology data reported by CNBC. More than half of that activity came through HELOCs and second-lien loans, with roughly 248,000 borrowers tapping about $25 billion that way — the strongest first-quarter second-lien volume in nearly 20 years — while another 234,000 homeowners pulled out about $22 billion via cash-out refinances, the highest first-quarter level since 2022. The surge is driven by a "lock-in effect," as homeowners use second liens to access equity without giving up low first-mortgage rates; average second-lien HELOC rates fell to 6.6% in March, their lowest since late 2022. By the third quarter, mortgage holders held a record $17.5 trillion in home equity, with nearly 2.4 million borrowers holding at least $1 million in equity. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/home-equity-borrow.html>, <https://www.housingwire.com/articles/ice-home-equity-lending/>, <https://nationalmortgageprofessional.com/news/second-lien-lending-hits-18-year-high-homeowners-protect-low-mortgage-rates>_
