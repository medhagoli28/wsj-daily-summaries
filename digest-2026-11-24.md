- **Jensen Huang Is Back at Nvidia. Major Investigations Still Surround Him.** — Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president and director on November 23, 2026, just 11 days after he resigned on November 12 amid revelations of extramarital affairs — including one with Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, who was described in reports as a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. Despite reinstating him, the board imposed new oversight: it formed a National Security and Export Control Committee led by director Suzanne Nora Johnson (with Stephen Neal and John Dabiri), hired law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct an independent investigation into whether company secrets or national security were compromised, and began a search for Nvidia's first Chief Compliance Officer, who will report directly to the board's governance committee. - **The Most Famous Problem in Math Was Just Solved by AI—and This Human** — Levent Alpöge, a number theorist at Anthropic (on leave from Harvard), announced on Friday, November 21, 2026, that he had produced a proof of the Riemann hypothesis—unsolved since 1859—working together with Anthropic's Claude AI; his result actually covers the broader generalized Riemann hypothesis. Rather than a conventional paper, Alpöge released the argument in the Lean proof-assistant language, backed by roughly 17 million lines of formally verified code, with an AI-generated explanation he says runs over 250,000 pages. The Riemann hypothesis is one of the six remaining Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize problems, carrying a $1 million reward, and mathematician Terence Tao had predicted in October 2026 that AI wouldn't crack it until around 2050. Independent verification of the claimed proof was still ongoing as of the announcement, and the mathematics community has reacted with a mix of astonishment and caution. _Sources: <https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/21/anthropic-worker-claims-to-have-solved-famed-167-year-old-maths-problem-with-help-of-ai>, <https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/anthropic-mathematician-claims-to-prove-riemann-hypothesis-most-famous-problem-in-all-of-math/>, <https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-03812-7>_
- **Cisco, OpenAI Launch Project to Safeguard Agentic AI** — Cisco and OpenAI announced an open-source project called Shirudo (Japanese for "shield") on November 23, 2026, aimed at making Linux infrastructure self-defending against cyberattacks using AI agents. The platform uses eBPF technology to let AI agents detect a vulnerability, build a protective "shield" inside the kernel, and test it against the exploit—without needing a source-code patch—before the shield is deployed, with humans setting the safety boundaries the agents must operate within. The goal is to shift security response from human-paced patching to automated, agent-driven defense, and the companies envision eventually distributing these shields alongside vulnerability disclosures to give organizations temporary protection until a permanent patch is available. SecurityWeek additionally frames the tool as specifically designed to shield unpatched Linux systems while patches are pending. _Sources: <https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m11/cisco-and-openai-launch-shirudo-a-new-project-to-safeguard-agentic-ai.html>, <https://www.securityweek.com/openai-cisco-unveil-open-source-tool-to-shield-unpatched-linux-systems/>_
- **U.S. Blacklists Alibaba-Owned Company Over Chip Curbs** — The U.S. Commerce Department added seven companies based in Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand to its export blacklist, including Apex Enterprise Solutions, a Singapore-based unit of Alibaba that procures computing capacity for the Chinese tech giant, over concerns that advanced Nvidia chips were being diverted to unauthorized users in China. The action, disclosed November 24, 2026, stems from an ongoing investigation into suspected chip diversion and means any exports, re-exports, or transfers of items subject to U.S. Export Administration Regulations to these firms now require a license. Also named was Singapore's Megaspeed, an Nvidia partner. Alibaba rejected the designation, calling Apex's inclusion "erroneous," stating the firm never diverted export-controlled advanced computing chips, and said it would "seek all legal avenues" to protect its rights and reputation. _Sources: <https://stockanalysis.com/news/us-blacklists-alibaba-unit-megaspeed/>, <https://www.tipranks.com/news/alibaba-unit-blacklisted-by-u-s-as-nvidia-faces-more-china-chip-scrutiny>, <https://invezz.com/sg/news/2026/11/24/alibaba-responds-to-chip-diversion-claims-as-us-blacklists-megaspeed-6-other-firms/>_

A second Kuiper launch in June 2025 was pushed back by United Launch Alliance due to weather, compounding delays already caused by faulty supplier components and limited rocket availability. Amazon has acknowledged production bottlenecks in its satellite manufacturing, even as it continues deploying batches from Florida to build out the broadband constellation meant to rival Starlink's dominance in satellite internet. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/10/amazon-kuiper-second-satellite-launch-delayed-by-ula-due-to-weather.html>, <https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/23/amazons-starlink-competitor-runs-into-production-delays/>, <https://www.engadget.com/science/space/amazons-starlink-rival-project-kuiper-is-reportedly-way-behind-schedule-153514471.html>_

## Markets & Finance

- **Compass Group Plans to Move Primary Listing to U.S.** — Compass Group, the British catering giant, has launched a shareholder consultation on moving its primary stock market listing from London to the United States, with the transition expected to complete in 2028 pending approval. The company said North America now generates roughly 75% of group underlying operating profit and employs about 325,000 people — more than half its global workforce — making a US listing a better fit with its largest client base and the world's deepest capital markets. Compass plans to retain a secondary listing in the UK and will keep its headquarters there. The move follows similar primary-listing shifts to the US by Flutter Entertainment, CRH, and Wise, adding to concerns about an exodus of major companies from the London Stock Exchange. _Sources: <https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/ftse-100-giant-compass-plans-to-move-primary-listing-to-us/>, <https://mlq.ai/news/compass-group-plans-to-move-its-primary-listing-to-the-us/>_

The rout deepened later in the month: Reuters reported Nvidia shares plunged further around November 20 amid a broader AI-sector sink, even as Anthropic's newly listed shares gave back most of their IPO gains. UBS characterized the move as a "positioning reset" rather than the start of an AI investment downturn, arguing underlying demand remains intact and still projecting about $1.5 trillion in AI-related spending by 2028. Other strategists framed it similarly as a realignment of valuations and expectations rather than a breakdown of the AI capex cycle itself. _Sources: <https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-stock-sell-off-positioning-155812283.html>, <https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/global-ai-stocks-sink-as-nvidia-crisis-deepens-sector-selloff-5021776>, <https://www.axios.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-ipo-nvidia-ai-tech-selloff>_
