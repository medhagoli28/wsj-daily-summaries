- **Jensen Is Back. But AI’s Problems Haven’t Gone Away.** — Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang returned to the top job this week after resigning days earlier amid revelations of extramarital affairs, including one with a Singapore-based executive reportedly scrutinized for possible ties to Chinese intelligence; interim CFO Colette Kress had briefly taken over before Huang's reinstatement, which sent Nvidia shares up more than 30%. Despite the stock pop, analysts say deeper structural worries remain unresolved, including heavy reliance on "circular financing" deals in which Nvidia extends credit or guarantees to customers like CoreWeave that then use the money to buy its chips, and rising bond yields among heavily indebted AI data-center firms. Nvidia shares had fallen roughly 33% from their October peak amid the scandal, and economists such as RSM's Joe Brusuelas have warned the episode could still trigger a broader unwinding of AI-related valuations even with Huang back at the helm. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/24/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-credit-markets.html>, <https://www.axios.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-jensen-huang-ai-bubble>, <https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20261124-jensen-huang-s-return-has-calmed-markets-but-fears-of-an-ai-bubble-still-run-deep>_
- **Anthropic Had the Biggest IPO Ever. The Trade Is Already Unraveling.** — Anthropic priced the largest IPO in history on November 18-19, 2026, selling shares at $83 each — above its $70-$78 target range — to raise over $81 billion and value the company near $1.5 trillion, before shares opened at $111.50 (up 34%) and briefly touched a near-$2 trillion market cap. The rally reversed quickly: by Friday, November 20, shares had fallen roughly 20% from that opening trade, pulling the implied valuation down to about $1.63 trillion, amid a broader tech selloff described as Wall Street's worst week since 2020. The slide drew scrutiny because the IPO price already marked a huge markup from Anthropic's last private valuation of $965 billion just months earlier, fueling investor doubts about whether the public price was sustainable. _Sources: <https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/21/anthropic-ipo-tech-selloff.html>, <https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/19/business/live-news/anthropic-ipo>, <https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/11/19/anthropic-raises-81-3bn-in-biggest-ipo-of-all-time>_
